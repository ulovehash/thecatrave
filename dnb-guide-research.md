## 4. Semantic kernel

**Primary intent:** explain what drum & bass is — its origin as the form jungle became after the mid-1990s split, how the sound is built, its subgenres, its people and institutions, and how it became a global genre. Reader job: someone who has heard "drum and bass" or "DnB" and wants the real picture, not a glossary.

**Proven / high-value language to represent naturally:** drum and bass, DnB / d&b / drum & bass, dnb music, what is drum and bass, drum and bass bpm (174 / 170–180), drum and bass subgenres, drum and bass artists, liquid drum and bass, neurofunk, techstep, jump up, what does dnb stand for / mean.

**Entities for topical completeness:**
- People: Goldie, Roni Size (& Reprazent), LTJ Bukem, Grooverider, Fabio, 4hero, Doc Scott, Photek, Dillinja, Ed Rush & Optical, Adam F, Andy C, Ray Keith, Krust, DJ Marky, High Contrast, Calibre, Noisia, Chase & Status, Pendulum, Netsky, Nia Archives.
- Labels / institutions: Metalheadz, Reinforced, Moving Shadow, No U-Turn, Good Looking, Full Cycle, RAM, V Recordings, Hospital, Shogun Audio, Critical, Exit.
- Places / scenes: London/Bristol origin; then continental Europe, Brazil (sambass / DJ Marky), Australia & New Zealand, South Africa, USA; Prague (Let It Roll), Moscow (Pirate Station), UK festival circuit (Hospitality, Rampage in Belgium).
- Technique / tech: Amen break, ~174 BPM, timestretching, the Akai S1000/S950 and later software, "two-step" vs "rolling" drums, sub-bass / Reese bass, half-time.
- Eras: 1991–93 hardcore→jungle precursors · 1994–95 the split · 1995–99 Metalheadz / intelligent / techstep / Bukem · 1997 Roni Size Mercury Prize · 2000s neurofunk + globalisation + jump-up · 2010s liquid/festival era + Rockwell/Noisia · 2020s streaming resurgence (Nia Archives, Bou).

**Comparison questions:** jungle vs drum and bass (editorial only); drum and bass vs dubstep (174 full-tempo vs 140 half-time — natural comparison in the "how the sound is built" section, links to `/dubstep-guide`).

**FAQ candidates (validate wording in the SERP during drafting):** What does DnB stand for? · What BPM is drum and bass? · Is drum and bass the same as jungle? · Is drum and bass EDM? · What are the main drum and bass subgenres?

**Excluded terms / intent (belong elsewhere or to no page):**
- 1991–1995 scene history in depth → **jungle-music-guide** (`#origins`, `#jungle-mania`, `#pioneers`). D&B gets one prologue section + link, per the plan.
- "dnb" bare acronym (bank / business) — not targeted.
- "how to make dnb" / production tutorials — a different intent; out of scope.
- Ragga jungle, Amen-break deep-dive as technique → jungle-music-guide and breakbeat-guide respectively; D&B links out.

### Proposed FAQ
1. What does DnB stand for? — DnB / d&b is drum and bass; a UK electronic genre at roughly 174 BPM.
2. What BPM is drum and bass? — usually 170–180, almost always ~174; the drums often imply half that.
3. Is drum and bass the same as jungle? — same root and tempo; jungle is the 1991–95 breakbeat-and-reggae scene, drum and bass is what a large part of it became. Link to jungle.
4. Is drum and bass EDM? — it predates the US "EDM" label and sits apart from it, though festivals now book both.
5. What are the main drum and bass subgenres? — liquid, jump-up, neurofunk, techstep, plus darkstep/drumfunk/halftime.
