Reviewed 2026-11-04 against `best-edm-festivals-las-vegas-draft.md`, `build-vegas-festivals-article.mjs` and the built `best-edm-festivals-las-vegas.html` (2,357 words). Stage 6 (topic validation) was run by a separate validator on 3 November 2026 ("Third wave: stage 6 verdict" in `TOPIC-DOSSIERS.md`). Every source below was opened with curl on 4 November 2026. Any later edit to the draft or builder needs a re-review.

## 1. Verdict

**Ready after revisions.** The page keeps to the stage 6 brief: EDC is one short linked entry (about 80 words over the H2 and H3, under the 150 cap), "edc las vegas" is not targeted, and the rest is other Vegas events and festivals within driving distance. Every confirmed 2027 date checks out against the festival's own site, except one omission.

- Escape Halloween has published 2027 dates (29 and 30 October 2027). The page says they are not announced.
- The H2 "What is the biggest EDM festival in Vegas?" is answered with an unsourced line ("the one large electronic music festival Las Vegas holds every year"). Insomniac's press page gives a sourced fact to use instead.

Two smaller factual fixes: System Overload runs two days, and Hollywood Park is in Inglewood. The H2 "EDM festivals Vegas fans can drive to in California" reads as forced. Its keyword can move into the intro.

## 2. What already works

- The intro answers the question in its first sentence and says plainly that the city's own calendar is short.
- The Breakaway entry is built from a dated press source (EDMTunes, 21 May 2026). Its line-up, venues, 21+ rule and Wynn Nightlife ticketing all match that source.
- The club section is attributed to Visit Las Vegas, and every venue named matches its list: Marquee, Palm Tree Beach Club, AYU, TAO Beach, OMNIA, Hakkasan, Zouk, LIV, XS and AREA15.
- Undated festivals show their 2026 date as a guide, and the criteria say so.
- "Not on this list" deals with UNITY and the Tomorrowland banner without overclaiming.
- No overlap with `best-edm-festivals-usa.html` beyond names. That page has EDC, Beyond Wonderland, CRSSD and HARD Summer as short entries. This page adds Breakaway, the clubs, Splash House, Nocturnal, Escape, Lightning in a Bottle, Goldrush and Das Energi.
- Both photographs are new to the site and openly licensed, with credits in the required form (Commons API, see §8).
