## Semantic kernel and approved structure

- Primary: Airbeat One Festival 2027, Neustadt-Glewe electronic festival.

## Fact and source ledger

- 7–11 July 2027, Australia theme, 25th anniversary and 24th edition: official 2027 information.
- First edition in 2002 and original Airbase One name: official history supported by contemporary festival histories.
- Mainstage added in 2010: documented history, retained without inflated significance.
- Stage identities: official stages page, treated as a current model rather than permanent names.
- Camping variations and Festival Plaza: official help centre; edition details remain subject to change.
