## Why this and not "winter festivals"

The four season phrases were rejected on 2026-09-22: their SERPs contain no
dance music (see `best-winter-music-festivals-research.md`). New Year's Eve
is the seasonal angle whose live SERP is **mostly electronic**, with more
demand than "winter music festivals" (100–1K).

## Candidate festivals (from sources above; dates to confirm for 2026–27)

- **US**: Countdown NYE (Insomniac, San Bernardino; "Dec 31 + Jan 1, 2027"
  per its site), Decadence (Denver/Phoenix; not yet sourced beyond
  keyword and iEDM), HiJinx (Philadelphia; Reddit says NYE this year),
  Lights All Night (Dallas Market Hall, late December), Eternal NYE
  (Orlando, bass), CRSSD Proper NYE (San Diego, "Proper NYE 26/27", tickets
  on sale 9 Sep), Forever Midnight (Insomniac, LA; created 2023), Jackpot NYE
  (Las Vegas, 26–27 Dec 2026, per MFW snippet).
- **Europe**: Awakenings NYE at the Gashouder, Amsterdam (Awakenings runs
  since 1997, Monumental Productions; NYE series dates Dec 28/29/31 per one
  search summary; confirm on awakenings.com). More European NYE events need
  a source pass (europa.tips "Europe's Best 2026 New Year Techno Festivals",
  found, not read).
- **New Zealand**: Rhythm and Vines (Waiohika Estate, Gisborne), Rhythm &
  Alps (near Wānaka) (Outlook Traveller; newzealand.com).
- **Australia**: Beyond the Valley, Lost Paradise, Field Day (Sydney, The
  Domain, New Year's Day, 20,000+) (Jones Around the World; livemusic.com.au).
- Elsewhere: not yet researched (e.g. India, Thailand, Mexico). Zamna shows
  in the SERP, but its NYE date is not confirmed.
