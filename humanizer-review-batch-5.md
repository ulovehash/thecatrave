## Observation, not a proposed edit: the festival guides share a template

Reading the festival guides one after another shows the same sentences in several of them:

- "Drum and bass is the part this site cares about most" appears in the Tomorrowland,
  Creamfields, EDC and Ultra guides.
- "For a listener who comes from breaks, jungle or drum and bass, that is the useful thing
  to know" appears in Tomorrowland, Creamfields and EDC.
- "not only under the fireworks" closes the music section of both Tomorrowland and EDC.
- "X is not a genre festival, and the Mainstage is not the whole of it" opens the music
  section of Tomorrowland and EDC (and Glastonbury has "Glastonbury is not a genre
  festival").

Each one is fine within its own page. Taken together, they are the kind of pattern
readers (and Google's own quality raters) notice across a site, because the same wording
recurs from page to page. Rewording them would change your standing voice for the festival
series, so the owner decides: keep them as a series signature, or vary the wording in each
guide.
