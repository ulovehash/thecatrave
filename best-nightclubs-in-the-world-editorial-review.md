## 1. Verdict
Ready after revisions: none blocking. Four Minor items below.

## 2. What already works
- Banner answers directly: DJ Mag 2026 ranks 1 to 5, the "public vote measures popularity" caveat and the read date.
- Every club carries only what DJ Mag's profile page states; the one club without a figure (fabric capacity) is marked "Not listed".
- The INA's rival list is named with its real status: voting and jury dates, jury 50%, 2026 list unpublished on 6 October 2026.
- No dash, no "not X but Y", no "full" or "complete" next to any set (grep on built text: 0 hits for complete, uninterrupted, in full, jungle, drum and bass, Wikipedia, matters, the point, em and en dashes; "full" 1 hit, "the full list" in a link sentence; two other hits removed).
- Owner decision of 6 October 2026: the world guide is not limited by the Europe guide's keyword claims and links to it by anchor text. The Europe guide is unedited.

## 3. Priority revisions
None Blocker or Major.
- Minor: "best clubs in the world" carries football-club volume in Keyword Planner, so the listener share of that 10K to 100K bucket is unknown.
- Minor: the catalogue has sets for only three of the twenty clubs (Club Space, D-Edge, Hï). The page says so and embeds nothing else.
- Minor: Drumsheds, The Warehouse Project, Bootshaus, Pacha, Amnesia, Tresor and Ministry of Sound facts come from the Europe guide's DJ Mag reads of 4 October 2026, not re-read here. The sources list says so.
- Minor: Hakkasan, Las Vegas appears in the 2026 list but its rank and profile were not read, so it has no rank in the page.
