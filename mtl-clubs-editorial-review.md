# Editorial review: Montreal clubs guide (/best-clubs-in-montreal)

Written 2026-11-09 by the agent that wrote the guide, so it is **not independent**. It covers the fact check, the humanizer pass and the SEO review. Stage 6 of TOPIC-RESEARCH.md was not run separately: the owner asked for the next three guides to be written one after another while using credits carefully ("next 3").

1. **Verdict:** ready after the owner reads it.
2. **What already works:**
   - The answer box names the after-hours club, five current house and techno rooms, the EDM room, the LGBTQ+ and alternative nights and the two outdoor festivals in one paragraph.
   - The history runs from Prohibition cabarets through the Limelight and the Foufs to Stereo, each step sourced.
   - The table keeps to dance-floor rooms and links each one to Google Maps and Resident Advisor through its RA club ID.
   - The FAQ answers the measured questions (best clubs, after hours, best dance club, biggest club, the Village, where the nightlife is, Stereo and alcohol, Piknic).
3. **Priority revisions:** none open. Fixed while writing:
   - Tiga's 2001 record is called a single, as Wikipedia has it.
   - Stereo is placed on the 6AM list as it is there: No. 28, between Coda in Toronto and Montreal's Beachclub.
   - Club Unity is not described as an LGBTQ+ club, and Le Belmont's street is not given: neither is in a source read.
   - Three figures that sat directly against a player were split from them with prose (Tiga, Piknic, Igloofest).
