- Read the music-side lists (Techno Mag, europenightlife, partiesnearme) in
  full for a four-source city cross-reference.
- Decide the scope: clubbing only (Berlin, Amsterdam, Tbilisi...) versus
  "party cities" (resorts). The volume is in the broader wording, but the site
  is music-led. Suggest clubbing cities with a short resort note.
- A one-line fact for each city (a club and a date) must come from sources.
  None have been gathered yet except those already in the city guides.
- No images sourced.

## Draft decision gate (for discussion, not final)

- **Primary intent.** Which European city to travel to for clubbing, judged
  by its clubs and scene rather than by bars and beaches.
- **Excluded.** Stag and hen trips, beach-resort party tourism, bar crawls.
- **Title direction.** "Best Clubbing Cities in Europe" (best clubbing cities
  in europe 1K–10K; best party cities in europe 1K–10K; best nightlife cities
  in europe 1K–10K).
- **Outline direction.** One H2 per city, each with its two or three clubs,
  its sound and a link to its guide. Order: Berlin, Amsterdam, London,
  Barcelona, Ibiza, Paris, Prague (best nightclubs in prague 1K–10K),
  Budapest, Krakow (best nightclubs krakow 1K–10K), Tbilisi, Lisbon, Belgrade.
  Then a comparison table (city, best for, standout club, closing time) and
  a FAQ ("biggest party cities in europe" 100–1K, "best clubbing cities in
  the world" PASF).
- Each written city guide gets a link from here and a link back.
