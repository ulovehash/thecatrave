## 3. Priority revisions

1. **Major. Wikipedia carries most of the history alone.** Attendance
   figures, the 1990 permit dispute, the 1997 driving ban, the 2002 airport,
   2017 death, 2020–21 unofficial attendance and the nonprofit year all rest on
   Wikipedia, which §2 allows as a discovery map only. history.burningman.org
   was checked: its timeline has year headers and images, no text. Next
   sources to try: the Burning Man census / AfterBurn reports (attendance),
   Burning Man Journal (2020–21, 2023), Brian Doherty's *This Is Burning Man*
   (1990, 1997). Until then keep the figures but add Wikipedia to Sources
   honestly, or drop the numbers that cannot be moved.
2. **Major. Four music claims are interpretation stated as fact.**
   See ledger rows 6–9. Qualify each, or source it. The music section is the
   part a DJ reader will check hardest.
3. **Major. The article stops instead of ending.** Controversy runs straight
   into the FAQ. Add a short closing section that pulls back to the music
   thread (what the edge-of-city history means for a listener, and where to
   hear it), which is also the natural place for the internal links in 5.
4. **Major. No internal links.** The draft links nowhere on the site. Natural
   routes: the Boiler Room guide (filmed sets as a format, Robot Heart's
   sunrise sets as the opposite of a booked set) and the Selector. Nothing
   links to UK genre guides honestly; do not force them.
5. **Minor. Three keyword-file terms are absent** from the text: "burning man
   art", "burning man history", "burning man principles". `audit-keywords.mjs`
   will fail. Either the H2s take the phrase naturally ("A short history of
   Burning Man" → "Burning Man history" reads worse; prefer a sentence in the
   body) or drop the terms from the file. Wording to be shown to the owner.
6. **Minor. FAQ "How long" and "Where" repeat the answer block** almost
   verbatim. Keep them (real queries) but add one fact each that the body does
   not give: arrival/Gate hours for "how long", the drive from Reno for
   "where".
