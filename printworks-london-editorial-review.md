# Printworks London editorial review

Reviewed 2026-10-05 against `printworks-london-draft.md`, `build-printworks-london-article.mjs` and the built `printworks-london.html`. Not independent: the same session gathered the evidence and wrote the page (Stage 6 not run by a second party).

## 1. Verdict

Publishable after the owner's read. The page is positioned as "closed and returning", not a visitor guide. Every dated claim traces to British Land, the Canada Water and Salamander consultation pages, Broadwick, Mixmag, DJ Mag, Resident Advisor, the Guardian or the BBC. The page says plainly that no reopening date exists in any source.

## 2. What works

- Head terms (printworks london, printworks) sit in the title, answer block and H2s.
- A dated reopening timeline table separates official facts (2024 application, September 2024 approval, May 2026 consultation) from hope.
- Drumsheds is covered with sourced facts only, and the BBC's "fills a gap" framing is labelled as the BBC's.
- One new Wikimedia Commons image, five sets filmed at Printworks.
