## Voting mechanics (DJ Mag voting kit and 2026 voting announcement)

- Poll started 1993 as an editorial list for the 100th issue; now in its 33rd year. DJ Mag: "the world's largest publicly voted music poll"; no nominations or eligibility criteria, voters nominate any five DJs.
- 2026 window: opened 8 July, closed 16 September 2026 (23:59 GMT); results due September/October 2026. Global link top100djsvote.djmag.com, separate China-only link (djmagvote.cn).
- Rules: one vote per person; every vote validated by DJ Mag; no incentives (free drinks, tickets, competition entries, exclusive content, merchandise, discounts); no PR teams canvassing in random places (streets, schools, shopping centres, sporting events); canvassing allowed only at events where the artist plays and aimed at their fans, and it must be registered in advance or the votes are invalid. Asking fans to vote is permitted: artists run campaigns and DJ Mag supplies a logo pack.
- Not found: how the five picks are weighted, how many votes the winner gets, total votes. DJ Mag does not print them on the pages read. Do not state any of these.
- Charity link: the 2025 and 2026 polls are in aid of Bridges for Music. No pledge figure verified; do not state one.
