# Editorial review: San Francisco clubs guide (/best-clubs-in-san-francisco)

Written 2026-11-09 by the agent that wrote the guide, so it is **not independent**. It covers the fact check, the humanizer pass and the SEO review. Stage 6 of TOPIC-RESEARCH.md was not run separately: the owner asked for the next three guides to be written one after another while using credits carefully ("next 3").

1. **Verdict:** ready after the owner reads it.
2. **What already works:**
   - The answer box names seven current house and techno rooms, the after-hours club, the alternative venue, the LGBTQ+ club and the history in one paragraph.
   - The history runs through buildings that still stand: the Trocadero building is now The Grand, and the Wicked reunion address is now The Great Northern.
   - The table keeps to dance-floor rooms and links each one to Google Maps and Resident Advisor where an RA club ID was found.
   - The FAQ answers the measured questions (best, biggest, most famous, techno, 18 and up, where the nightlife is, hi-NRG).
3. **Priority revisions:** none open. Fixed while writing:
   - Halcyon's area was corrected to SoMa (314 11th Street), and its opening is now sourced to Resident Advisor's 2016 report.
   - Oasis: RA reported in July 2025 that it would close on 1 January 2026. Later reports (SF Standard, 17 July 2026) say it was saved and reopened, so it stays, with the reopening stated and sourced.
   - The Great Northern is kept in the Mission, as Time Out lists it. RA places it in SoMa; the guide follows Time Out.

5. **Humanizer pass** (on the draft, 2026-11-09):
   - No "this guide" and no description of the page itself.
   - Sets are introduced by club and DJ, with nothing about their length.
   - No drum and bass, jungle or breaks asides.
   - Nothing teaches production; nothing hands out files.
6. **SEO:**
   - New page. `keywords/sf-clubs.json` holds the Keyword Planner terms (All locations, English, read 2026-11-09). Each targeted term appears verbatim.
   - Rejected: adult clubs, bars and jazz bars (bar intent), bowling, comedy, private and social clubs, Club Fugazi (theatre), and live-music venues such as Bimbo's 365.
   - Title: "Best Clubs in San Francisco: Nightclubs for House and Techno".
7. **Coverage gaps:**
   - Halcyon and Oasis have no RA club ID found, so their table rows carry Maps links only through the guide text, not the directory.
   - Temple, Madrone Art Bar and Power Exchange are on Time Out's list and left out: rooftop lounge, art bar and adult venue.
8. **Media:**
