# Editorial review: best-bass-music-festivals

Reviewed 2026-11-04 against `best-bass-music-festivals-draft.md`, `build-bass-festivals-article.mjs`, `keywords/best-bass-music-festivals.json`, `media/best-bass-music-festivals.json` and the built `best-bass-music-festivals.html` (2,108 words before fixes). The reviewer did not write the guide or gather its research, so the review is independent. Stage 6 (topic validation) was run by a separate validator on 3 November 2026 ("Third wave: stage 6 verdict", `TOPIC-DOSSIERS.md`). The owner read has not been run. The blockers and clear fixes below were applied the same day and the page was rebuilt with `node build-bass-festivals-article.mjs` only. Any later edit needs a re-review.

## 1. Verdict

**Ready after revisions (revisions applied).** Every 2027 date checked out against the festival's own page, and so did the 2026 dates for the two festivals that have not announced. One problem blocked publication: the page targeted a taken keyword through a Bass Canyon FAQ. Also fixed: one unsourced superlative, two lines of process wording, an overstated length for Outlook, a caption claim the file page does not support, and missing links on entries the USA and Canada pages already cover. One coverage question stays open for the owner (§9): Europe has three entries against six in North America.

## 2. What already works

- The page follows the stage 6 verdict. It does not define bass music and links to `/bass-music-guide` in the first paragraph. Dubstep festivals get their own H2. Drum and bass gets one linked line (Rampage Open Air, to `/best-drum-and-bass-festivals`). The only other drum and bass mention is Bassfest's own genre list.
- The dates are honest. Confirmed dates are separated from the Bassfest ticket-seller listing, which is marked "listed", and from the unannounced Lost Lands and Wakaan, which show their 2026 dates as a guide.
- The Outlook set comes from the festival's own channel and is the only embed specific to this guide. The Lost Lands photograph is new to the site.
- The calendar note on the late-July overlap (Outlook and Shambhala, then Sound Haven) gives the reader something a plain list does not.
- No em or en dashes, no "full" or "complete" set wording, no production teaching, no Wikipedia in the body.

- FAQ. Cut: "### When is Bass Canyon Music Festival 2027? / Bass Canyon 2027 is on 13 to 15 August at The Gorge Amphitheatre in George, Washington."
- Keyword file: "bass canyon music festival" moved from `terms` to `rejected`, with the reason recorded.

New: "Bass Canyon is held at The Gorge Amphitheatre in George, Washington. The 2027 edition is confirmed for 13 to 15 August. For how it sits among the bigger American festivals, see [the best EDM festivals in the USA](/best-edm-festivals-usa)."

New: "Most of the North American festivals here are camping weekends, from British Columbia to Tennessee." (The USA link moves to the Bass Canyon entry, B1.)

**M2. The entries already covered elsewhere were not linked.** The verdict names an overlap with the USA page (Lost Lands, Bass Canyon) and the Canada page (Bass Coast, Shambhala). Lost Lands and Shambhala had no link. (Applied.)
- Lost Lands, appended: "It also appears in [the best EDM festivals in the USA](/best-edm-festivals-usa)."

- Shambhala, appended: "More on Shambhala and Bass Coast is in [the best EDM festivals in Canada](/best-edm-festivals-canada)."

## 6. Coverage gaps

- The verdict asks for the UK and Europe to be weighted equally with the US. The page has three European entries against seven North American ones (four in the US and Canada section, plus Lost Lands and Wakaan). This is an editorial weakness, but no sourced European candidate was found in this review to fill it. Dub Camp and NASS are excluded for stated reasons. Owner's call (§9).
- No festival outside Europe and North America appears, although the verdict says worldwide. The title and intro do not claim worldwide coverage, so nothing published is false.
