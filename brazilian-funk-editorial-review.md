# Editorial review: Brazilian funk guide (/brazilian-funk-guide)

Written 2026-11-09 by the agent that wrote the guide, so it is **not independent**. It covers the fact check, the humanizer pass and the SEO review. Stage 6 of TOPIC-RESEARCH.md was not run separately: the owner asked for three guides to be written one after another while using credits carefully ("lets create first 3, not in parallel, write them 1 by 1 use creadits conciuosly").

1. **Verdict:** ready after the owner reads it.
2. **What already works:**
   - The answer box gives all three names (Brazilian funk, funk carioca, baile funk) and says that in Brazil "baile funk" means the party.
   - The page separates the music from the party, a distinction the PAA questions keep asking about.
   - The phonk section answers "Brazilian phonk" searches without targeting them, and says plainly that the label is not used in Brazil.
   - A styles table covers nine subgenres, each with a place, a date and a record.
3. **Priority revisions:** none open. Fixed while writing:
   - Removed claims with no source in the notes: "dozens" of strains, DJ Marlboro as "the DJ most associated", "anthropologist" for Hermano Vianna, Tetine as a "London duo", Haaksman as a "German DJ" who "ran" Man Recordings, Diplo "more than anyone", KondZilla as "the biggest channel", Mr. Catra as a best-known MC, a reason for the phonk label, a shared feel with gqom, and Ludmilla as funk melody.
   - "Bum Bum Tam Tam travelled furthest" became "one of the best known abroad".
   - "São Paulo" is credited to The Weeknd and Anitta, matching the official upload.
   - Two sources conflicted with Wikipedia and were left out: Bandcamp Daily's "Rio's Baile do Mandela" (Wikipedia: Praia Grande), and the Guardian's claim that bruxaria gave birth to phonk.
   - Anitta's "Envolver" is reggaeton, so it is not called funk.

5. **Humanizer pass** (on the draft, 2026-11-09):
   - No "this guide" and no description of the page itself.
   - The sets are introduced by place, with nothing about their length.
   - There are no drum and bass, jungle or breaks asides.
   - No PAA question is used as an empty heading.
6. **SEO:**
   - New page. `keywords/brazilian-funk.json` targets 10 terms, with Keyword Planner ranges and Ahrefs US figures kept apart.
   - Rejected: "brazilian phonk" (phonk intent), loop, sample and vocal samples (file intent), "brazil funk power", "brazilian funk music 2022" and a soul compilation.
   - The FAQ answers eight PAA and PASF questions in full.
   - Title: "What Is Brazilian Funk? Baile Funk, Funk Carioca and Songs".
7. **Coverage gaps:**
   - "It's Automatic" and "Ela É do Tipo" have no official upload that was found, so they are named without a player.
   - Badsista's Boiler Room set is already on the Primavera Sound page, so it is not reused here.
8. **Media:**

   - Thirteen records from official, label or artist channels and three Boiler Room sets (Furacão 2000, Rennan da Penha and DJ K). Each passed oEmbed and was checked unused by any other guide.
