# Pacha Ibiza editorial review

Reviewed 2026-10-04 against `pacha-ibiza-draft.md`, `build-pacha-ibiza-article.mjs` and the built `pacha-ibiza.html`. Not independent: the same session gathered the evidence and wrote the page (Stage 6 not run by a second party).

## 1. Verdict

Publishable after the owner's read. Every dated or numeric claim traces to pacha.com, Ibiza Spotlight, Gray Area, Ibiza Rocks, Resident Advisor or FIVE Holdings' own release, each opened on 2026-10-04. Where sources are silent or disagree (guest list, a club-stated capacity, table minimum spend, the origin of the name, 2027 dates), the page says so.

## 2. What works

- Head terms (pacha ibiza, pacha ibiza club, pacha) sit in the title, answer block, hero and H2s.
- Dress code is quoted verbatim from the club's FAQ, with the looser Ibiza Spotlight reading attributed and set beside it.
- The closing week is a table of dates, lineups and price ranges as pacha.com listed them on the read date.
- Three new Wikimedia Commons images, three sets filmed at Pacha.

## 3. Priority revisions

1. Meta description is the approved Stage 1 text (123 characters). Title is 50 characters.
2. Two of the three sets (Sven Vath Cocoon 2018, Solomun and Andhim 2014) are also on the Ibiza clubs guide. They are the only catalogue sets titled as filmed at Pacha. Owner to confirm the reuse. The Vagabundos 2016 set is new to this page.
3. The Ibiza clubs guide does not link back to this page. Adding a link is an edit to published copy and needs shown wording first.
4. The prices are listed ranges. pacha.com does not say what sits inside them, and the page says so.
