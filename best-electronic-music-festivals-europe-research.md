## Intent and evidence

Current choice and comparison, not a history hub. Owner-supplied Ahrefs,
September 2026, global: "electronic music festivals in europe" 90,
"electronic music festivals europe" 80, about 220 across viable Europe terms,
about 100 more in stale year variants. The parent category (EDM festivals
5,000, techno festival 3,100, electronic music festivals 1,100) is not
addressable by a Europe-only page and is not claimed. The page's main value is
internal: it routes readers to the nine European festival guides on the site.

## Selection criteria (shown on the page)

1. Electronic music is the core of the programme.
2. In DJ Mag's Top 100 Festivals 2026 (ranks from
   `djmag-festival-priorities.md`).
3. A European home edition held recently and expected in 2027.
4. A distinct sound, setting or format.

Fourteen pass: Tomorrowland, Untold, Parookaville, Creamfields, Ultra Europe,
Mysteryland, Defqon.1, Awakenings, Dekmantel, Time Warp, Kappa FuturFestival,
Sónar, Monegros, Boomtown. Named as excluded, with reasons: Primavera Sound,
Glastonbury, EXIT, Sziget, Amsterdam Dance Event, franchise editions.

## Maintenance (a condition of publishing)

- Registered in `festival-editions.mjs` with `ends: null`, so every build
  prints a reminder while any 2027 date is unannounced.
- October to November 2026: pre-season pass; confirm Tomorrowland, Dekmantel,
  Monegros and EXIT's 2027 home.
- On each official date or ticket-phase announcement: update that row and the
  festival's section.
- January 2027: full accuracy pass before the main booking period.
- September 2027: roll the page to 2028 (title, H1, table, `festival-editions`).
- `date` in the generator changes only when facts change, so the visible
  update date stays truthful.
- Owner of the updates: the site owner, who will update it each year
  (confirmed 2026-09-22).

## Smaller festivals section (added 2026-09-22, owner request)

The owner asked for Garbicz and other underground festivals "even though they
can have low SEO volume". They are held to their own test, stated on the page:
electronic core, a 2026 edition held, and named in at least two independent
roundups of underground or boutique European festivals. Roundups read:
Telekom Electronic Beats "20 European techno festivals" (2019), Dirty Disco
"Top 10 boutique festivals Europe" (2026), Fresh Island "Best underground
techno festivals in Europe 2026", Internet Tattoo "Europe's coolest
underground electronic dance music festivals", and a DJ Mag 2026 poll summary
(Houghton's highest climber award).
