## Intent and evidence

Current choice and comparison, not a history hub. Owner-supplied Ahrefs,
September 2026, global: "electronic music festivals in europe" 90,
"electronic music festivals europe" 80, about 220 across viable Europe terms,
about 100 more in stale year variants. The parent category (EDM festivals
5,000, techno festival 3,100, electronic music festivals 1,100) is not
addressable by a Europe-only page and is not claimed. The page's main value is
internal: it routes readers to the nine European festival guides on the site.

## Selection criteria (shown on the page)

1. Electronic music is the core of the programme.
2. In DJ Mag's Top 100 Festivals 2026 (ranks from
   `djmag-festival-priorities.md`).
3. A European home edition held recently and expected in 2027.
4. A distinct sound, setting or format.

Fourteen pass: Tomorrowland, Untold, Parookaville, Creamfields, Ultra Europe,
Mysteryland, Defqon.1, Awakenings, Dekmantel, Time Warp, Kappa FuturFestival,
Sónar, Monegros, Boomtown. Named as excluded, with reasons: Primavera Sound,
Glastonbury, EXIT, Sziget, Amsterdam Dance Event, franchise editions.
