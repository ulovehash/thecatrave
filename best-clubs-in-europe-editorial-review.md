# Best Clubs in Europe editorial review

Reviewed 2026-10-04 against `best-clubs-in-europe-draft.md`, `build-best-clubs-in-europe-article.mjs` and the built `best-clubs-in-europe.html`. Not independent: the same session gathered the evidence (Stage 6 not run by a second party).

## 1. Verdict
Ready after revisions: none blocking. Four Minor items below, left as is pending owner view.

## 2. What already works
- Direct answer in the banner: the DJ Mag 2026 anchors (ranks 1, 4, 8, 11, 13, 21), the count (23 clubs, ten countries) and the read date.
- Every club entry carries only what a named source states; gaps are said (the [UNVRS] capacity is omitted, Prozak is flagged as thinly documented, Lux Frágil's profile is flagged as five years old).
- The DJ Mag table is labelled a subset and says which ranks were not checked.
- The guide stays Europe-only. "Best clubs in the world" and "best nightclubs in the world" get one short FAQ answer.
- The NTS Tresor set is labelled a listening guide, not a recording from the club.

## 3. Priority revisions
None Blocker or Major.
- Minor: Rex Club (DJ Mag 2022 profile plus Visit Paris Region) and Lux Frágil (2021 profile) rest on older profiles. Re-check both first at the next refresh.
- Minor: [UNVRS] capacity conflicts at source (profile page versus results article), so no figure is printed.
- Minor: the meta description approved in Stage 1 said "sets to hear from each room". Only five catalogue sets exist across six clubs, so it reads "from several rooms". Owner to confirm.
- Minor: the Sven Väth Pacha 2018 set also sits on the Pacha and Ibiza guides. Reused deliberately (the catalogue has few club-recorded sets) and disclosed in `media/best-clubs-in-europe.json`.

## 5. SEO preservation
New page. Title 50 characters. Description 105 characters. Every term in `keywords/best-clubs-in-europe.json` appears on the page (audit-keywords passes). Excluded on purpose: "best clubs in europe 2026", "best house music clubs", "best techno clubs in europe", and the no-data phrases. Not targeted, because `best-clubbing-cities-in-europe` owns them: "best clubbing cities in europe" and "best party cities in europe". FAQ text matches the FAQPage data.

## 6. Coverage gaps
No Berghain or Pacha-specific set exists in the catalogue for the Berghain entry. Only DJ Mag-listed entries were checked for rank; ranks 31 to 100 beyond those named were not read. Dates for 2027 club seasons are not claimed.

## 7. Cuts or merges
Unsupported claims cut (section 4). FAQ answers repeat earlier facts, which is acceptable for FAQ.

## 8. Media actions
Seven images, all Wikimedia Commons, new to this page, none borrowed from another guide, local webp (1200 and 320, each under 300 KB), credited in captions as `Photograph: <author>, <licence>.` Rejected: a US roadside bar mislabelled Rex Club, a sculpture for Shelter, and the Drumsheds photographs (author already used on the Bristol guide). Five catalogue sets in three collections (Spain, UK, Tresor) and one owner mix via `ownSetListening`. The `iT3Ebi3ZHiA` Pacha set is not used because its title stresses completeness. `media/best-clubs-in-europe.json` exists with no required records.
