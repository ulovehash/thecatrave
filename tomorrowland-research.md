## Proposed structure

- **URL** `/tomorrowland-festival` — the form with the weakest SERP and the
  highest TP (32,000, the head's own page). `/what-is-tomorrowland` would copy
  the Burning Man pattern but that SERP has no article slot.
- **Title** Tomorrowland Festival: What It Is, Where It Happens, Why It Got So Big
- **H1** Tomorrowland Festival
- **Meta** Belgium's festival in a park in Boom, its editions abroad, how many
  people go, who owns it, and what the music is under the mainstage.
- One disambiguation line under the H1: not the Disney land, not the 2015 film.

1. **Answer block** (`infoBanner`, "WHAT IS TOMORROWLAND"): what, where,
   when (month, no year), how many people, 80–120 words. Carries "what is",
   "where is", "location", "when is".
2. **Where Tomorrowland happens.** Boom, Belgium; how to picture the site.
   Carries "belgium", "location".
   - H3 Tomorrowland Winter
   - H3 Tomorrowland Thailand
   - H3 Tomorrowland Brasil and TomorrowWorld (past editions, only if sourced)
   - H3 Tomorrowland in the USA — the Insomniac tie-up; answers PAA "Is
     Tomorrowland coming to the USA?" without dates that go stale.
3. **How big it is.** Attendance, capacity, sell-outs, the livestream. Table of
   attendance by year (eventplanner.net's 8,700 in 2005 to 600,000, checked
   against Wikipedia). Answers PAA capacity, "biggest festival", "vs
   Glastonbury". Carries "attendance", "how many people go".
4. **History and who owns it.** First edition to now in eras, the founders,
   the company behind it; the 2025 stage fire as one paragraph. Carries "who
   owns", "fire".
5. **Why it got so famous.** The stage design, the aftermovies, the global
   ticket model. Answers the two PAA "why so famous / what is so special".
6. **What the music actually is.** The site's own angle and the only section
   that earns the author's standing: mainstage big-room EDM, then what the
   hosted stages carry. H3s only for names a source confirms (candidates from
   also-talk-about: Dimitri Vegas & Like Mike, Martin Garrix, Armin van Buuren,
   Swedish House Mafia, David Guetta, Carl Cox). Roster via `FIGURES.md`.
7. **Essential listening**: full-bleed block, sets from Tomorrowland's own
   channel.
8. **FAQ** (five): ticket cost, how many days, when, is it coming to the USA,
   what a DJ is paid (only if a source exists; otherwise dropped).
9. Author, Sources, Bandcamp, Read Next (Burning Man first: same series).

## Excluded intents

- Lineups, set times, dated editions, live-stream schedules: official site,
  stale within a year.
- Tickets and packages: one FAQ answer and a link.
- Disney park and film: one disambiguation line, nothing else.
- Travel and hotels.

## Open before drafting

- **Fit.** Tomorrowland is mainstage EDM, far from breakbeat and jungle.
  Section 6 is where the owner's voice lives; whether there is a real angle
  (what plays off the mainstage) needs a source, not memory. Owner's call.
- **Market.** 90% of demand is outside the US. The SERPs are US; a GB or BE
  SERP for "tomorrowland festival" is not checked.
- **Stage 6** validation not run.
- Every fact in §2–§5 needs a source: dates of editions, founders, owner
  company, the USA deal, attendance by year, the fire.
- Images: openly licensed Commons photographs of the Boom site, new to this
  site. Nothing borrowed from other guides.
- `keywords/tomorrowland.json` to be written at implementation, from the table
  above.
