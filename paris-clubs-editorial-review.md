# Paris clubs: editorial-review record

## Verdict

SEO/user-intent revision implemented in the generator and draft on 6 October 2026. This is not an independent full factual re-review of the entire published guide.

## What already works

The guide distinguishes Rex Club, Essaim, Badaboum and La Station and preserves the histories of Le Palace and Les Bains Douches. Those four current venues are covered by Resident Advisor's 2026 guide: https://ra.co/guides/clubs-in-paris . Existing URL, anchors, media and artist promotion remain.

## Priority revisions

The open-club comparison moved before closed-club history. Title, description, H1 and introduction now answer the measured current-clubs intent. A route to current listings was added after the comparison.
