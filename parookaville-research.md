31,000, the Netherlands 1,300, France 1,100, the US 700, Austria 500,
Switzerland 450, Belgium 300, the UK 250, Spain 200, Italy 150.

- **German-language share.** Germany alone is 84% of the demand; with Austria
  and Switzerland, the German-speaking countries carry about 31,950, or 86%.

  every question form is German (wo findet parookaville statt, parookaville
  wie viele besucher, wann ist parookaville, parookaville was ist das).
- **English-language demand.** The US and UK together are about 950 (2.6%).
  The only English-form listener term with volume is "parookaville festival",
  900 worldwide, of which 600 is German searchers typing the English word order,
  50 the US.
- **What an English page can realistically earn.** The best evidence is what
  the English Wikipedia article earns now: about 470 visits a month, almost all
  outside Germany (Finland 250 at position 1, Italy 66, the Czech Republic 45,
  Romania 38, Portugal 37, the US 18). In Germany it sits at position 15 for the
  head term and gets nothing, while the German article takes 831 a month from
  the same query at position 3. Google DE serves German pages to German
  searchers. So the realistic ceiling for this page is in the low hundreds of
  visits a month, from non-German markets and the English "festival" form, not
  a share of the 31,000. A German-language page would be a different decision,
  and is the owner's to make.

## Decisions

- **URL** `/parookaville-festival`. The head term is navigational (the
  official site takes 24,866 a month from it; TP 6,200) and German-served.
  "parookaville festival" is the only English-form listener term with volume
  (900 worldwide, 600 DE, TP 2,700 in DE), and its top ten has no article, with
  the weakest pages at 0 to 3 RD. It is the Creamfields pattern.
  `/what-is-parookaville` would be the Burning Man pattern, but no English
  "what is" form registers; the German "parookaville was ist das" is 150.
- **Title** Parookaville Festival: Where It Is, Who Runs It, the Music (58).
- **H1** Parookaville Festival.
- **Meta** Where Parookaville happens on an old RAF airbase at Weeze, how three
  friends built a festival staged as a city, how many people go, who runs it,
  and what plays.

Structure, mirroring Creamfields:

1. Answer block ("What is Parookaville"): what, where, when, how big, founders.
2. Where Parookaville happens: Weeze Airport, the RAF Laarbruch base, the
   hangars and shelters used as stages, mid-July, over-18s.
3. How big Parookaville is: daily attendance, admissions table (de.wikipedia,
   qualified as admissions), the documented 70,000-capacity Mainstage audience
   area in 2024, and the 2019 livestream.
4. A short history, and who owns Parookaville: the founders, 2015, the 2017
   mud, the 2018 drought, Superstruct in 2019, 2020 and 2021.
5. Why Parookaville is famous: the city and Bill Parooka, the weddings, the
   stages, the awards and the DJ Mag chart.
6. What the music actually is: big-room EDM and trance, techno, hardstyle and
   hardcore, German acts; drum and bass as a guest, in the owner's voice.
7. Hearing Parookaville from home: Essential listening, W&W 2022 and Steve
   Aoki 2025.
8. FAQ (six, from PAA and question terms).
9. Author, Sources, Bandcamp, Read Next.

## Excluded intents

- Line-ups, set times, dated editions, livestream schedules: official site.
- Tickets: one concise current FAQ answer that points to the official 2027 sale;
  no price table or transactional guide.
- Travel, shuttles, camping, outfits.
- German-language phrasing of the evergreen questions: answered in English.

## Maintenance fact-check, 2026-09-15

- Superstruct Entertainment owns Parookaville; KKR acquired Superstruct in 2024
  and CVC invested alongside it. Parookaville GmbH remains the organiser, whose
  current imprint names Bernd Dicks and Johannes Bergers as representatives.
- Meyer Sound describes the 2024 Mainstage audience area as having a 70,000
  capacity. The earlier 45,000 claim is not retained.
- The official 2027 ticket page requires personalised visas, and the official
  Future City page says a digital ticket is accepted at the entrance. Entry is
  18-plus and identity is checked.
- The 2027 ticket sale is open; day visas go on sale on 4 October 2026. The
  stale 2025 third-party prices are removed.
- The sold-out 2026 edition reported 225,000 admissions across three show days
  and more than 300 acts. The article now describes the completed edition in the
  past tense.
