## Differentiation

- The instrumentals first: tempo, drum language, the tools, eskibeat and
  devil mixes, producers as well as MCs.
- Pirate radio, crews, clashes, Channel U and raves as the scene's
  infrastructure.
- The naming argument, sourced to Wiley's own 2003 Hyperdub interview.
- A comparison table: grime, UK garage, dubstep, UK drill, UK rap.
- The disputes: first record, "godfather", East London only.
- Thirteen exact records and two catalogue radio sets embedded in place.
