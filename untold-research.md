## The language question, and why it differs from Parookaville

The bare head "untold" is 29,000 a month worldwide, 14,000 of it in Romania,
and it collides: Dracula Untold (film, 65,000 global) and Club de Nuit Untold
(Armaf perfume, 29,000 global) sit in the same word. Its Romanian tail is
Romanian-language (bilete untold, program untold 2026, unde se tine untold,
cat costa un bilet la untold).

"untold festival" is different. It is an English word order with no collision,
11,000 worldwide, and Romania is only 1,000 of it (12-month average). The rest
is spread across Europe and North America. Latest-month figures from the
English Wikipedia article's rankings (September, just after the August
festival, so above the averages): Romania 3,100, Italy 2,000, Poland 2,000,
the UK 2,000, Spain 1,600, the US 900, Canada 900, Mexico 600, the
Netherlands 500, Norway 450. These are foreigners looking up a festival they
might travel to, in English, which is what an English page serves. Parookaville
was 86% German-language demand; Untold's English form is international.

## Facts gathered so far (en.wikipedia, fetched 2026-09-14)

Founded 2015, organised by the SHARE Cluj-Napoca Federation, in the year
Cluj-Napoca was European Youth Capital. Cluj Arena (main stage), BTarena
(Galaxy Stage), Central Park. Close to homes and exempt from the curfew, cited.
Best Major Festival, European Festival Awards 2015, cited. Attendance table:
2015 240,000; 2016 300,000; 2017 330,000; 2024 427,000; 2025 470,000; 2026
500,000 (admissions over four days). Sister events: Neversea (Constanța,
2017), Massif (Poiana Brașov, 2023), Untold Dubai (February 2024), Kapital
(Bucharest, 2025). A 2019 study on noise, waste, traffic and cost of living,
cited. Search summaries also give 2019 370,000 and 2023 over 420,000; not yet
checked against a source.

## Proposed decisions

- **URL** `/untold-festival`. The only clean English form; the bare "untold"
  collides with a film and a perfume and is Romanian-served. No article in
  the US or UK top ten; the weakest page in the US has 0 RD. The Creamfields
  and Parookaville pattern.
- **Title** Untold Festival: Where It Is, How Big, and the Music (52).
- **H1** Untold Festival.
- **Meta** Where Untold happens in Cluj-Napoca, how a European Youth Capital
  party became Romania's biggest festival, how many people go, who runs it,
  and what plays.

## Excluded intents

- Line-ups, program, dates, dated editions: official site.
- Tickets and prices: at most one dated FAQ answer.
- Travel, accommodation, what to bring.
- Romanian-language phrasing: answered in English.
- Dracula Untold, Club de Nuit Untold: collisions.
