## Facts gathered so far (to be fact-checked before drafting)

- Official: Barcelona 17, 18, 19 June 2027 (sonar.es/en header, read
  2026-09-14). Venue for 2027 not stated.
- Official: 2026 took place entirely at Fira Gran Via; stages SonarVillage,
  SonarClub, SonarCar, SonarHall, SonarPark, SonarLab. "Over 100 different
  editions worldwide" (sonar.es "What is Sónar").
- Wikipedia: founded 1994 by Ricard Robles, Enric Palau, Sergio Caballero;
  By Day at CCCB, By Night at Apolo (1994); Poble Espanyol 1995–96; Mar Bella
  1997; By Night to Fira Gran Via 2001; By Day to Fira Montjuïc 2013.
  Attendance ~6,000 (1994) to 126,000 (2018). Superstruct majority 2018; KKR
  bought Superstruct 2024.
- Press: 161,000 in 2025 (Mixmag Italy, gearnews); 150,000 in 2026 (weraveyou,
  Beats of Africa). Over 50 artists withdrew from 2025 over KKR (DJ Mag); the
  founders left in October 2025; François Jozic (Brunch Elektronik, Centris
  Group, which also runs OFFSónar) CEO (DJ Mag, IQ, Ara).
- Kraftwerk's first show in 12 years, 1998, Mar Bella (IMS, we-heart).
  Laurent Garnier at the first edition and since (sonar.es 2023 artist page).
- Unverified, left out until sourced: Laurent Garnier "sunrise set" date,
  Chic 2006, the 8,000 first-year figure (spanishexpress contradicts
  Wikipedia's ~6,000).
