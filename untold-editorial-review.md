Draft: `untold-draft.md`, 2026-09-14. Research and approved structure:
`untold-research.md`; keywords `keywords/untold.json`; media `media/untold.json`.
Structure approved by the owner on 2026-09-14, with the keyword map and the
2027 section. Stage 6 validation (`TOPIC-RESEARCH.md`) was not run.

## 1. Verdict

Ready after revisions. The revisions below were applied to the draft before
layout; none is open.

## 2. What already works

- The answer block states what, where, when, how big and who runs it in one
  paragraph, and the next edition's dates sit in the first section, where the
  largest dated query (untold 2027, 1,100) lands.
- Admissions are explained as a day-by-day count, with the daily figures that
  add up to the 2026 total, rather than printed as a head count.
- The criticism (fenced park, hospital, Court of Auditors, the 2019 study) is in
  the history, not buried: a guide that only praised Untold would read as the
  festival's own copy.
- The music section separates the stadium's EDM and pop headliners from the
  rooms around them, by stage, and says plainly how little drum and bass there
  is, without forcing it into the listening.

## 3. Priority revisions (applied)

- Major: "a prize that had never gone to a festival in its first year" was the
  festival's own claim, stated as fact. Now attributed: "the first time, the
  festival says".
- Major: "The Berlin duo Pan-Pot" came from memory with no fetched source.
  "Berlin" removed.
- Major: the Read Next paragraph called Ultra Europe an August trip; it is in
  July. Rewritten.
- Minor: "come back every summer since" ignored 2020; "bar the pandemic" added.
- Minor: "Romanian artists open the main stage each day" overstated Wikipedia's
  "usually". Softened.
- Minor: "Untold made Romania a destination" was unattributed opinion. Now
  credited to Mixmag and DJ Mag, as Wikipedia's reception section does.
- Minor: Omnipass described as "a network of benefits for students", closer to
  the source than "student discounts"; the 2027 sales sentence no longer implies
  a date ("within weeks") the source does not give.
