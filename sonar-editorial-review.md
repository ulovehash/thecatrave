# Sónar Festival Barcelona: editorial review

Reviewed 2026-09-14 against `sonar-draft.md`, `sonar-research.md`,
`keywords/sonar.json` and `media/sonar.json`, per `ARTICLE-EDITORIAL-REVIEW.md`.
New page: no Search Console preservation inventory exists.

## 1. Verdict

Ready for layout. No blocker. Two claims rest on a single secondary source and
are phrased as reported figures.

## 2. What already works

- The answer block gives what, when, where, how big, who owns it and the next
  dates in five sentences.
- The ownership story (Superstruct 2018, KKR 2024, the 2025 withdrawals, the
  founders leaving in October 2025) is told plainly, with dates, and ties to
  OFFSónar through Centris Group rather than sitting as a news aside.
- The 2025 attendance figure is broken down so it is not compared naively with
  2026 (161,000 included 42,000 at Sónar Week events).
- Players are from official broadcast partners only; fan uploads with more
  views are named in `media/sonar.json` and rejected.

## 3. Priority revisions

None open. Applied while drafting:

- **Major, fixed.** Kraftwerk 1998 "first show in 12 years" (spanishexpress,
  IMS summary) cut: Kraftwerk headlined Tribal Gathering in May 1997, their
  first festival set. Wikipedia's Kraftwerk 3D show in 2013 is used instead.
- **Major, fixed.** Sónar+D "13th edition" in 2026 (We Rave You) cut: counting
  from 2013 it would be the 14th.
- **Minor, fixed.** "Red Bull Music Academy at Sónar since 1998" reduced to the
  unambiguous claim, its 20th anniversary there in 2018.
- **Minor, fixed.** We Rave You gives Sónar 2027 as 17 to 20 June; the official
  site says 17, 18, 19 June. The official dates are used.
