## 3. Priority revisions (applied)

- Major: the official schedule has not announced the 2027 Chicago dates. The
  likely 29 July to 1 August window is explicitly an editorial projection,
  and the aggregator citation was removed.
- Major: the contradictory 400,000 to more than 500,000 attendance range and
  the "eight or nine stages" wording were replaced with nearly 460,000
  admissions in 2026, a 115,000 daily cap, and the official eight-stage count.
- Major: the location section now includes the current 2023-2032 Grant Park
  agreement and its five-year extension option.
- Major: Chicago is described as the flagship and permanent US edition, not
  the original location. The first show is placed precisely at Compton Terrace
  in Chandler, in the Phoenix metropolitan area.
- Major: Stockholm's run and 2024 pause are dated accurately, without calling
  it an indefinite hiatus. The table now gives Santiago's and Berlin's current
  venues.
- Major: the 2003 revival tour is distinguished from the planned 2004 tour,
  which was cancelled before it began.
- Major: current official, municipal and first-party sources replace Wikipedia
  as the main evidence for dates, attendance, capacity, producer, control and
  international locations.
- Major: a caption named the Willis Tower from the photograph, with no source;
  now "the Chicago skyline".
- Major: the official address page returned 403; "lollapalooza address" was
  dropped from the keyword map rather than an address being guessed.
- Minor: "as the festival calls itself" had no source; now "as it is often
  shortened". The Perry's Stage sentence now rests only on the SIDEPIECE
  upload's title and the festival's own posts.
- Minor: a table of editions added where the site-wide audit requires one and
  the section needs it.
