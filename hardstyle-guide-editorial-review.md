## 2. What already works

- The opening defines hardstyle through an audible feature, the distorted pitched kick, before expanding into BPM, reverse bass and arrangement.
- The history avoids naming one inventor or first record. It presents hardstyle as an exchange between Dutch, Belgian, Italian and German hard-dance scenes.
- The early, melodic, euphoric and raw periods are demonstrated with exact records placed beside the relevant prose.
- The comparison with hardcore, techno and hard techno is concise and does not flatten the neighbouring genres into synonyms.
- Current festival claims stay general, while dated schedules remain with official organisers.
