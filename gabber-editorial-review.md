# Editorial review: gabber guide (/gabber-guide)

Written 2026-11-09 by the agent that wrote the guide, so it is **not independent**. It covers the fact check, the humanizer pass and the SEO review. Stage 6 of TOPIC-RESEARCH.md was not run separately: the owner asked for three guides to be written one after another while using credits carefully ("lets create first 3, not in parallel, write them 1 by 1 use creadits conciuosly").

1. **Verdict:** ready after the owner reads it.
2. **What already works:**
   - The answer box defines gabber in one sentence, with place, decade, tempo and sound.
   - Hardcore is the family and gabber the Dutch form inside it. The page says so early, which answers "is gabber the same as hardcore techno".
   - Happy hardcore has its own section, covering the British and the Dutch strands, so it needs no separate page.
   - The anti-racism section rests on Mokum catalogue numbers, not on general claims.
3. **Priority revisions:** none open. Fixed while writing:
   - Removed four phrases with no source: "Kiosk Radio in Brussels", "mixing hardcore with industrial music", "frenchcore grew out of the French free-party scene" and a flourish on the Lenny Dee set.
   - Removed an introduction paragraph that described the page.
   - Fixed a Guardian URL that had been written from memory.
   - Rewrote all five image alt texts after looking at the images.

5. **Humanizer pass** (on the draft, 2026-11-09):
   - No "this guide" and no description of the page itself.
   - The sets are introduced by place and date, with nothing about their length.
   - There are no drum and bass, jungle or breaks asides.
6. **SEO:**
   - New page. `keywords/gabber.json` targets 14 terms, with Keyword Planner ranges and Ahrefs US figures kept apart.
   - Rejected as production or file intent: VST, samples, downloads and "fl studio frenchcore".
   - Dropped because they only read as search strings: "gabber 90s", "90's happy hardcore" and "dutch gabba".
   - The FAQ answers the four PAA questions in full.
   - Title: "What Is Gabber? Gabber Music, Hardcore Techno and Happy Hardcore".
