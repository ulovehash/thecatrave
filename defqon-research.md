## The language question

"defqon 1" is 11,000 a month worldwide: Germany 4,100, the Netherlands 1,800,
France 1,000, the US 700, Belgium 500, the UK 450, Austria 400, Spain 300,
Switzerland 250, Sweden 200. "defqon" is 9,600: the Netherlands 4,000, Germany
1,900, France 600, the US 500, Belgium 400, Australia 350, the UK 200.

Most of it is the festival's name typed in Germany and the Netherlands, and the
Dutch long tail is Dutch-language (defqon afgelast 1,500, defqon 1 hitte 800,
wanneer is defqon 2026 800, waar is defqon 400, rellen defqon 250, hoeveel
bezoekers defqon 200). English-language informational demand is small: see
stage 3. The pattern is Parookaville's, not Untold's.

The words "defcon 1" and "defqon festival" collide with DEFCON, the US military
readiness level ("defcon 1" 9,700 in the US alone).

## Facts gathered so far (search summaries; to be checked against sources)

- Founded 2003 by Q-dance; hardstyle and its offshoots (rawstyle, hardcore,
  uptempo, early and euphoric hardstyle).
- Almeerderstrand, Almere, before 2011; since 2011 the event site beside
  Walibi Holland at Biddinghuizen, Flevoland. The first edition's location is
  given differently in summaries: unverified.
- Four days, camping from Thursday; more than 300 artists; more than 250,000
  visitors from over 100 countries (Wikipedia via search); RED is the main
  stage; the Sunday Endshow.
- Editions in Australia and Chile in the past.
- 2026: cancelled on Friday 26 June after the Netherlands' first code red
  warning for extreme heat (DJ Mag, NL Times, EDM.com, Hardstyle.com); the
  Endshow was filmed without an audience and published (DJ Mag).
- 2027: 24 to 27 June, Biddinghuizen (EDM Identity, We Rave You, EDMTunes,
  Edmtrain); 2026 ticket holders offered 2027 tickets or a refund. The
  Q-dance 2027 page renders nothing without JavaScript: read it in the browser
  before drafting.
