## 3. Priority revisions (applied)

- **Blocker, removed**: "the Las Vegas Motor Speedway, north of the Strip".
  Written from memory; no source read gives the location relative to the Strip.
- **Blocker, removed**: "the festival's philosophy, All Are Welcome Here". Seen
  only in a search-result summary of Insomniac's page, which returned 403.
- **Blocker, removed**: the 2026 theme "celebrated three decades of rave
  culture". Only in an Instagram caption; My Modern Met's wording ("a tribute to
  the path the festival has been on") does not say it.
- **Major, corrected**: Sub Focus's kineticFIELD set called his "main stage
  debut". A search summary said so; no source read confirms it. The stage itself
  is confirmed by the title on his own channel.
- **Major, corrected**: EDC UK "first in London, then Milton Keynes". Wikipedia
  says London in 2013 and "the National Bowl in Milton Keynes, UK for the third
  time" in 2015, which do not agree; the draft now says only that it was at the
  National Bowl by its third year.
- **Major, superseded 2026-09-15**: "largest electronic dance music festival
  worldwide" was first attributed to Wikipedia, then removed in favour of
  Insomniac's narrower North American claim.
- **Minor, corrected**: Brazil's circuit given by its Wikipedia name, not a
  nickname from memory; "bleachers" to "stands", feet given with metres.
- **Blocker, corrected 2026-09-15**: 525,000 was assigned to 2023. The published
  record now gives 2024; the reported 2023 estimate was 520,000.
- **Blocker, corrected 2026-09-15**: the origin story credited Stephen
  Hauptfuhr alone and treated Chino as settled. It now credits Hauptfuhr and
  Gary Richards, avoids forcing Chino versus Pomona, and separates the early
  events from Insomniac's documented 1997 lineage.
- **Blocker, corrected 2026-09-15**: the first Bassrush Arena was dated to 2001.
  Insomniac's official timeline dates it to 2002.
- **Major, corrected 2026-09-15**: the Live Nation paragraph called the 2013
  deal "not an acquisition" despite undisclosed terms and contemporaneous
  reports of an investment. It now states only what the companies put on the
  record: a partnership, Live Nation resources and retained Insomniac creative
  control.
- **Major, corrected 2026-09-15**: worldwide superlatives and conflicting stage
  counts were replaced with Insomniac's current description, largest dance
  music festival in North America, and its official 2026 count of more than
  450 artists across 18 music areas and art cars.
