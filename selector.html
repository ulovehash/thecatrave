What this is.

The Selector is for when you want a DJ set but don't want to choose one. Press the button and it plays a full set picked at random from a catalogue of 38,037 sets from 19 channels. Press it again for another. Nothing is saved and nothing is personalised.

Two filters narrow the pool before it picks. Source limits it to one channel, so you can pull a random Boiler Room set, or an HÖR set, or one from NTS Radio, Keep Hush, The Lot Radio, Kiosk Radio. Popular only keeps just the most-liked third of the catalogue, which is the closest thing here to a "best sets" list. Genre covers house, techno, drum and bass, dubstep, UK garage, jungle, electro, breakbeat, hip-hop and disco where a set is tagged.

The pool is the long-form uploads of Boiler Room, HÖR, NTS Radio, Keep Hush, The Lot Radio, Kiosk Radio, Cercle, Rinse FM, Mixmag, My Analog Journal, Seoul Community Radio, Bangkok Community Radio, The Mudd Show, Boxout.fm, MixMix TV, STVOL TV, L'Atelier de Musique, Beatport, Elevator Music: real sets rather than clips, refreshed weekly. For the history behind the music, read the drum and bass guide, the jungle guide, the breakbeat guide and the dubstep guide.

Questions about picking a set.

How does The Selector pick a set?

You press one button and it plays a full DJ set chosen at random from its catalogue of 38,037 sets from 19 channels. Press again for another. There is no algorithm learning your taste and no history kept.

Can I get a random Boiler Room set?

Yes. Open the Source filter and choose Boiler Room, then press the button to shuffle only Boiler Room sets. The same works for HÖR, NTS, Beatport, The Lot Radio, Rinse FM, Kiosk Radio, Cercle and every other channel in the list.

How do I find the best or most popular sets?

Turn on Popular only. It limits the pool to the most-liked third of the catalogue before the random pick, so every result is a set that already has a big audience. Combine it with a Source or Genre filter for the most-liked techno set, the most-liked HÖR set, and so on.

Which channels does it pull from?

Boiler Room, HÖR, NTS Radio, Keep Hush, The Lot Radio, Kiosk Radio, Cercle, Rinse FM, Mixmag, My Analog Journal, Seoul Community Radio, Bangkok Community Radio, The Mudd Show, Boxout.fm, MixMix TV, STVOL TV, L'Atelier de Musique, Beatport, Elevator Music. Every one is a public YouTube channel and the catalogue is refreshed weekly.

Can I filter by genre?

Yes, where a set is tagged. The Genre row covers house, techno, drum and bass, dubstep, UK garage, jungle, electro, breakbeat, hip-hop, disco and more. Sets that could not be tagged sit in an "untagged" bucket you can include or exclude.

Do the sets play here or on YouTube?

Each pick embeds the full set on this page with a link out to watch it on YouTube. Nothing is downloaded and there is no account, sign-up or install.
