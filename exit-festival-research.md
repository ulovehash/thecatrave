## Semantic kernel and approved structure

- Primary: EXIT Festival history and current status.
- Supporting: EXIT Festival Novi Sad, Serbia, Petrovaradin Fortress, Dance Arena, global tour.

- Excluded: invented 2027 Novi Sad dates, treating every EXIT-produced event as the same festival, political claims stated without attribution.

## Fact and source ledger

- Founded in 2000 as a student movement and moved to Petrovaradin in 2001: official history, supported by independent and academic sources.
- 2025 announced as final Serbian edition under current political conditions: EXIT statement; retain as the organiser's position.
- More than 200,000 reported in 2025: organiser's cumulative attendance language, not simultaneous or unique attendance.
- No 2026 Novi Sad edition: official site and tour schedule.
- Global tour and Starlight: official current pages. EXIT explicitly says these are new festivals, not one relocation.
- Criticism of EXIT's earlier relationship with Serbian authorities: Le Monde reporting; summarize without taking an unsupported side.
