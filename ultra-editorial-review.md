## 2. What already works

- The answer block carries what, where, when, how many and who in one
  paragraph; the H1 and title carry "ultra music festival", the body carries
  "ultra miami", its parent topic.
- Ultra Europe has its own H2, as the owner decided, with the forms the research
  found in each market: ultra europe, ultra split (Croatia), ultra croatia,
  ultra festival croatia (GB), ultra europe split, ultra festival split (DE).
- The history is told through the city: the licence, the 2014 incident, the
  Virginia Key year, the 55,000 cap. That is the part the competing guides skip.
- The music section argues from the bill, not from the site's taste: LTJ Bukem
  in 1999, Andy C in 2001, Pendulum closing 2016, each from Miami New Times or
  Wikipedia, and the Pendulum set is playable from the band's own channel.
- Essential listening carries the festival's two most-watched full sets that
  also sit on a best-performances list, per the series rule.
- The Ultra Records collision is answered in one line.

## 3. Priority revisions (applied)

- **Blocker, removed**: "Bicentennial Park, a short way up Biscayne Boulevard".
  Geography from memory; no source read places it.
- **Blocker, corrected**: "In 2011 it sold out for the first time". Ultra's own
  press release, cited by Wikipedia, dates the first sell-out to 2010 (two days,
  over 100,000). The history, the table and the 2011 caption now say 2010, "by
  its own count"; Wikipedia's 93,000 for 2010 is shown in the table.
- **Blocker, removed**: "In 2009 ... Ultra sold more tickets than any single
  event in the city of Miami had before." Wikipedia's prose gives this for 2009
  at "over 70,000", its table gives 70,000 for 2008 and 85,000 for 2009. The row
  and the sentence are gone.
- **Blocker, replaced**: "a Main Stage slot at Ultra has long been where the
  biggest names choose to play something new". A generalisation with nothing
  under it. Replaced with the four premieres the sources name: Avicii's finished
  "Levels" (2011), Eric Prydz's Holo and Jack Ü's debut (2014), deadmau5's Cube
  v3 (2019).
- **Major, corrected**: "The Live Stage carries the bands: The Killers ... The
  Cure". Miami New Times has The Cure on the Main Stage; only the 2012 bill is
  tied to "the old Live Stage". Reworded.
- **Major, corrected**: Hardwell "second on the same list". The Miami New Times
  list is chronological, not ranked; now "also on that list".
- **Major, corrected**: "the Ultra festival Croatia has hosted every July since
  2013". 2020 and 2021 were postponed; "the two pandemic years aside" added.
- **Major, corrected**: "thousands walked the William Powell Bridge".
  Wikipedia says "many"; the number came from a search summary. Now "many
  people".
- **Major, corrected**: Knife Party turned into Pendulum "halfway through ... on
  the Main Stage". The Miami New Times text read is cut off before either
  detail; now "turned it into a Pendulum set".
- **Major, removed**: "Ultra's most famous rival for the title of the world's
  biggest electronic music festival". No source; the Tomorrowland and EDC links
  now describe those festivals instead.
- **Major, removed**: "the best known of its editions abroad" (answer block),
  "the edition most people abroad have heard of" (Ultra Europe section), "Ultra
  Europe above all" (introduction) and "has moved more than most festivals'
  do". Unmeasured comparisons; the last three were caught on the built page at
  visual QA.
- **Minor, corrected**: DJ Craze dropped from the drum and bass paragraph (his
  genre was not in a source read); The Prodigy called breakbeat, not drum and
  bass; the FAQ ticket answer keeps only what Miami New Times says ("from
  $479.15"), not the tier and VIP prices from a search summary.
- **Minor, corrected**: 1999 "in the week of the Winter Music Conference" and
  the FAQ's "roots in the parties around" it. Only "held alongside" is sourced.
- **Blocker, corrected 15 September**: `165,000 tickets` and unqualified people
  counts. The number is now identified as reported admissions summed across
  three days, about 55,000 per day, not unique visitors or tickets.
- **Blocker, updated 15 September**: the 2022 Bayfront agreement through 2027
  was superseded in April 2026. The page now records the agreement that can run
  through 2046, its four-fifths revocation route and five-year reviews.
- **Blocker, corrected 15 September**: the unsupported claim that Faibisch and
  Russakoff alone own the festival. The copy now distinguishes Event
  Entertainment Group as organiser, its current officers, and Ultra Enterprises
  as the documented intellectual-property holder without inventing shares.
- **Major, corrected 15 September**: the Swedish House Mafia sentence now
  distinguishes the Saturday takeover, Eric Prydz joining the group's set and
  Armand Van Helden's separate guest segment.
- **Major, updated 15 September**: the stale fixed 2026 ticket price was removed.
  The FAQ links to the official live GA/VIP status for 2027.
