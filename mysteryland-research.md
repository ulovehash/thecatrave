## Facts settled

- Haarlemmermeer since **2003**, not 2002: the Dutch Wikipedia and the
  official site agree (2002 was Ruigoord); the English Wikipedia's "2002"
  is the Floriade's year, not the festival's.
- Visitors: 100,000+ in 2019 (Dutch Wikipedia, official site in 2020); the
  official site now says 125,000+. The page gives both, attributed.
- 2027: "27-28-29 August 2027", official FAQ, read raw on 14 September 2026.
- 2025 finale: drones, fireworks, pyrotechnics, 3D visuals, lasers (press
  release). No drone count is given there, so none is on the page.
- Owner: ID&T; SFX 75% 2013, LiveStyle 2016, Superstruct 2021, KKR 2024
  (English Wikipedia, ID&T).

## Decisions

- **URL** `/mysteryland-festival`: the festival form has TP 21,000 and the
  weakest SERPs (listings at 0 to 6 RD, no article in NL).
- **Title** Mysteryland Festival: Where It Is, Its History, and 2027 (55).
  The year changes every year with the next edition.
- **H1** Mysteryland Festival.
