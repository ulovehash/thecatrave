## Sourced facts (to be fact-checked again at review)

- **Boiler Room.** March 2010, London. Blaise Bellville asked Thristian Richards
  to livestream a mix for a magazine; weekly Ustream show; Floating Points played
  the second edition, recording lost. Berlin, LA, São Paulo within a year.
  DJ EZ 24-hour set, Feb 2016, £60,000+ for Cancer Research UK. 4:3 launched
  2018. First Boiler Room Festival 2019, Peckham. Bought by DICE 2021
  (Billboard), sold to Superstruct Entertainment January 2025 (Billboard); KKR
  ownership prompted artist cancellations. Bellville resigned December 2025
  (mneemo, verify). Charli xcx, Brooklyn, Feb 2024: 25,000+ RSVPs (V Magazine).
  Source: Wikipedia and its citations.
- **Rinse FM.** Pirate, first broadcast September 1994, Ingram House, Tower
  Hamlets; Geeneus and Slimzee. Jungle, then garage 1998–99, then grime and
  dubstep. Slimzee ASBO 2005. Online streaming 2006. Community licence June
  2010, legal on 106.8 FM from 7 February 2011. Rinse France February 2014.
  Source: Wikipedia.
- **Red Light Radio.** Amsterdam, 2010–2020, studio in a former brothel on
  Oudekerksplein; daily shows ended with a final broadcast 27 June 2020. Source:
  Wikipedia, RA news 72625.
- **NTS.** April 2011, Hackney; Femi Adeyemi (with Sean McAuliffe). £5,000
  budget. Name from Adeyemi's blog "Nuts To Soup". Two channels. Universal Music
  Group took a 25–50% stake June 2023. Source: Wikipedia.
- **Mixmag, The Lab.** Launched 2012 per Mixmag search snippets, but a "10
  years of The Lab" feature is dated 2021. **Conflict, unresolved** (mixmag.net
  returned 403).
- **The Lot Radio.** 2016, François Vaxelaire, a reclaimed 20-foot shipping
  container at 17 Nassau Avenue, Williamsburg; studio on one side, coffee and
  beer kiosk on the other; 24/7. Source: Wikipedia, RA news 33465.
- **Cercle.** Derek Barbolla, Paris, 2016. First broadcast Møme at the Eiffel
  Tower, October 2016; earlier Monday streams from his apartment, then a
  sandwich-shop basement, a club, a barge. Château de Chambord, Giza, Petra,
  Salar de Uyuni. Cercle Records September 2020. Source: Wikipedia, Billboard
  2019 interview.
- **Kiosk Radio.** 2017, five friends, a wooden kiosk in the Parc Royal,
  Brussels, after a City of Brussels call for proposals. Source: search
  snippets of kioskradio.com/about and VICE; verify.
- **Keep Hush.** Fred Conybeare and Freddy Masters; started as a blog; first
  broadcast from a Soho office basement studio; members propose the line-ups;
  UK funky, garage, grime (Sherelle, Slimzee, P Money). **Founding year not
  found.** Source: DMY.
- **HÖR.** August 2019, Kreuzberg, Ori Itshaky and Doron Charly Mastey (the duo
  TV.OUT, from Tel Aviv). White-tiled room, green neon, booth at the window.
  November 2023 backlash over two stopped sets. Acquired by 99Solutions June
  2026 (RA news 85346). Sources: search snippets (ADAM Audio, Goethe-Institut),
  Billboard Canada, RA; Goethe-Institut article "Live from the storage room"
  returned 403, read it through the browser at fact-check.
- **2020.** Berlin clubs closed 13 March 2020; United We Stream from 18 March
  2020, nightly at 7pm; within a year 2,000+ artists from ~450 locations in ~100
  cities (gl-systemhaus, Clubcommission). Boiler Room "Streaming From
  Isolation" fundraising series (Disclosure 10 April 2020, Peggy Gou 27 April).

## Outline

1. Hero plus direct answer (80–120 words): a London broadcaster that since
   2010 has filmed DJs with the crowd standing behind the decks, streamed live
   and archived on YouTube; a "Boiler Room set" and a "Boiler Room party" mean
   that format.
2. H2 **Before Boiler Room**: pirate radio as the first "platform" (Rinse from
   1994, online from 2006), online radio with a room attached (Red Light Radio
   2010). The owner's jungle angle lives here.
3. H2 **Boiler Room, 2010 to 2020**: the Ustream webcam, Floating Points' lost
   second show, the cities, DJ EZ's 24 hours, 4:3, the first festival. What the
   camera position changed.
4. H2 **Radio with a camera: NTS, The Lot, Kiosk**: the 24/7 model, rooms as
   landmarks (a container, a park kiosk), the community-radio network in the
   catalogue (Seoul, Bangkok, Hong Kong, Manila).
5. H2 **Other formats: The Lab, Cercle, Keep Hush**: the magazine office booth,
   the heritage site and drone, the members club.
6. H2 **2020, when every club became a stream**: United We Stream, Streaming
   From Isolation, HÖR's white-tiled room finding its audience, Red Light Radio
   closing.
7. H2 **Who owns the platforms now**: DICE and Superstruct, UMG in NTS,
   99Solutions and HÖR. Controversy only as far as it changed ownership or
   line-ups (owner to decide, see below).
8. H2 **The platforms by the numbers**: the catalogue table above as
   `articleTable`, with the Cercle finding. Links to the Selector.
9. FAQ (five, per `faqHasQuestions`): What is a Boiler Room set? / What is a
   Boiler Room party? / Who owns Boiler Room? / Why is Boiler Room so popular? /
   What does NTS stand for?
