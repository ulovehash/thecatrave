Review date: 21 September 2026.

## 1. Verdict

Ready to publish.

## 2. What already works

- The opening answers the broad recommendation query without pretending that follower count measures quality.
- The page has one clear reader job and sends discovery-method questions to the existing guide.
- The electronic section matches the site's expertise without replacing the broader keyword intent.
- Ownership of both thecatrave playlists is disclosed in the headings and prose.
- Every entry explains a distinct use rather than repeating “good for discovering music”.

This is a new URL, so no legacy copy or anchors require preservation. Keep the exact primary phrase in the title, H1 and direct answer. Preserve the separation from `how to find new music on Spotify`.

None material. A “most followed” table would answer a different query and is intentionally absent.

- Reject a separate popular-playlists section: it would repeat RouteNote's ranking intent.

- Reject follower figures: they change quickly and do not drive the judgement.
