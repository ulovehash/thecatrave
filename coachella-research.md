## Stage 1: web search first

"what is Coachella festival", "Coachella festival guide history Empire Polo
Club Indio": en.wikipedia (Coachella, Coachella Festival line-ups, one article
per edition such as Coachella 2024, 2025, 2026), EBSCO Research Starters,
AEG Worldwide, the official site (festival info, documentary), Britannica
("Coachella Valley Festival"), Palm Springs Life on the Empire Polo Club,
Concert Archives.

Facts from those summaries, to be checked against sources before drafting:
Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival, founded 1999 by Paul Tollett and
Rick Van Santen; organised by Goldenvoice, part of AEG Presents since 2001;
first edition October 1999, two days, Beck and Rage Against the Machine, more
than 25,000 people, lost money; Empire Polo Club in Indio, California, founded
1987 by Alexander Haagen; Goldenvoice's 1993 Pearl Jam show there; two
consecutive three-day weekends in April; about 245,000 across both weekends in
2025; rock, pop, indie, hip-hop, Latin and electronic, with electronic music
better represented than at most festivals.
