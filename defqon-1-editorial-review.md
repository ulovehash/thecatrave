# Defqon.1 2027 editorial review

Reviewed 2026-10-05 against `defqon-1-draft.md`, `build-defqon-1-article.mjs` and the built `defqon-1.html`. Not independent: the same session gathered the evidence (Stage 6 not run by a second party).

## 1. Verdict
Ready after revisions: none blocking. Five Minor items below, left as is pending owner view.

## 2. What already works
- Direct answer in the banner: 2027 dates, the 2026 cancellation, the sales stages, and what the official pages do not state (prices, lineup).
- Every date and price names its source and read date (5 October 2026). Where the official pages are silent, the page says so instead of filling the gap.
- The 2026 cancellation is attributed to the official update and two press reports; an unverified claim of unrest was left out.
- The Australia question is answered from one dated source and described as a venue withdrawal, not a law.

## 3. Priority revisions
None Blocker or Major.
- Minor: no 2027 ticket price, stage genre list, lineup or 2027 travel page was stated on the pages read. Re-read when Q-dance publishes them (the sales stages start 27 October 2026).
- Minor: the travel and shuttle section is the 2026 pattern, labelled as such. The 2026 travel page has a date typo ("June 26th" for Thursday) that is not copied.
- Minor: opening and camping times were read on Q-dance information pages without a year marker on the page; the draft says "the pages read give".
- Minor: three images, not four or five. The Commons originals could not be saved as files (shell offline, Chrome blocked repeat downloads), so the UV (1090 px wide) and endshow (1200 px) images were captured at screen size. The Red 2023 image was downloaded as the 1280 px Commons thumbnail. A fourth image (weekend bracelets, KaiKnight2) was skipped because a screen capture of a portrait file would be too small.
- Minor: none of the three catalogue sets was recorded at Defqon.1. The page says so.
