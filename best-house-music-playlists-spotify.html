House music playlists

The best house music playlists on Spotify

7 min read

Updated 8 October 2026

Ten focused house selections, plus two disclosed thecatrave playlists that connect house and techno to a wider electronic record bag.

Best house music playlists on Spotify: The best house music playlist on Spotify depends on which part of house you want. Housewerk is the broad current feed, Spotify's 90s House Classics covers crossover records, Soulful House keeps vocals and live musicianship in view, and Afro House Pulse focuses on African artists and the wider global scene. Label and artist playlists offer stronger identities. The final two selections are my own multi-genre playlists, included for listeners who want house and techno in a wider electronic context.

Choose the lane before the playlist.

"House playlist" can mean Chicago records, vocal garage, deep house, tech house or a pop-dance feed with a four-on-the-floor kick. The playlists below cover those differences, with previews to help you choose.

The best house playlists on Spotify come from its editorial team, established labels and working DJs. A house music playlist on Spotify can change quickly, so their order here follows the kind of listening they support. For a broad cross-genre list, use The Best Spotify Playlists. For the history behind the music, start with What Is House Music?.

How these house playlists were chosen.

Each playlist has an identifiable curator. Some rotate through new releases. Others work as durable archives. The notes below describe what the playlist is useful for and where its boundaries become loose.

House foundations and soulful records.

Spotify

90s House Classics

Spotify's 90s House Classics is the quickest route into the decade when house crossed from clubs into pop without losing the swing of its drums. Crystal Waters, CeCe Peniston, Robin S and the French touch sit together. The result is a broad archive of crossover house, with Chicago only one part of the story.

Open full playlist on Spotify ↗

Spotify

Soulful House

Soulful House puts singers, live-feeling keys and long-running producers such as Louie Vega and Miguel Migs ahead of harder drops. Use it when "deep house" playlists feel too anonymous or when the vocal carries the record instead of supplying a short hook.

Open full playlist on Spotify ↗

Honey Dijon

Housewerk NYE by Honey Dijon

Honey Dijon's Housewerk NYE playlist stretches from disco and funk into current house. Her selection puts those older records alongside the house she plays today.

Current house and label filters.

Spotify

Housewerk

Housewerk is Spotify's large current-house feed. It moves quickly and favours accessible club records, so it is useful for hearing what has crossed into the platform's main electronic lane. The breadth also means it has less of a single personality than the label and artist playlists below.

Open full playlist on Spotify ↗

Defected Records

Defected 2026

Defected's annual playlist follows the label's vocal, soulful and club-facing house world. It is a release feed: new records appear beside artists and styles the label has supported for years.

Open full playlist on Spotify ↗

Toolroom Records

Toolroom Tech House

Toolroom Tech House stays close to the label's functional club sound: firm low end, clear builds and tracks designed to mix cleanly. It is the most focused choice here for current tech house.

Open full playlist on Spotify ↗

Selected

Deep House 2026 by Selected

Selected's playlist covers polished deep and vocal house with a strong new-release bias. It sits closer to contemporary streaming-era dance music than to the jazzier 1990s meaning of deep house.

Melodic, global and artist-curated routes.

Anjunadeep

Anjunadeep 2026

Anjunadeep's annual list is a direct feed from the label's melodic house catalogue. Long chords, gradual builds and detailed production make it useful for listeners who want atmosphere without leaving club tempo.

Open full playlist on Spotify ↗

Spotify

Afro House Pulse

Spotify describes Afro House Pulse as music from Africa and around the globe. The playlist is a better entry point than lists that use "Afro" as a vague mood tag, although the category still covers several countries, languages and local traditions that should not be treated as one scene.

Open full playlist on Spotify ↗

Bicep

Feel My Bicep

Feel My Bicep is closer to a DJ's working record bag than a narrow house playlist. House sits beside breaks, electro and older electronic records. Follow it when the connections around house are more useful than a clean genre boundary.

Which house playlist should you choose?

Start with 90s House Classics for familiar foundations, Soulful House for vocals, Honey Dijon for disco lineage and Housewerk for a broad current scan. Defected and Toolroom provide clear label filters. Selected and Anjunadeep cover smoother modern production, Afro House Pulse opens a global route, and Feel My Bicep loosens the genre boundary. Choose Rare Electronic Music for a harder cross-genre path through house and techno, or Emotional Electronic Music for melodic club music with a wider stylistic range.

No single playlist represents house music. The genre grew through local clubs, labels and DJs, and its Spotify categories still overlap. Saving two lists from different curators shows the differences quickly.
