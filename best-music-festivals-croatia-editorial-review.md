# Editorial review: best-music-festivals-croatia

Reviewed 2026-11-02 against `best-music-festivals-croatia-draft.md`, `build-croatia-festivals-article.mjs` and the built `best-music-festivals-croatia.html`. The reviewer is independent of the writer. The draft changed while this review was running: the L106 "How to choose" opener was reworded. Everything below is checked against the draft as it stood at about 12:02 on 2 November 2026, so any later edit needs a re-review. Stage 6 (owner read) has not been run.

## 1. Verdict

**Blocked until revisions.** The page is well organised by place (Split, Zrće, Tisno, elsewhere) and its dates agree across the table, answer box and FAQ. Three problems block publication:

- The headline claim that Ultra Europe is "the biggest electronic festival in the country" has no source.
- The Sonus line says something its source does not say.
- How to choose describes two overlapping festivals as following one another.

Several genre labels and one superlative about Dekmantel Selectors are also unsourced. The page is free of dnb angles, asides and dnb links: the Hospitality and Outlook entries are factual entries for festivals that take place in Croatia, which the house rule allows.

## 2. What already works

- The H2 questions match what readers search for (biggest, Zrće, Tisno). The keyword audit passes, including "festivals in croatia" (1000) and "best festivals in croatia" (10).
- The Tisno section turns a confusing calendar into a list a reader can choose from.
- The Not-on-this-list entries (Sonus, Defected Croatia) answer the questions readers will actually ask in 2027.
- No em or en dashes, no process commentary, no whole-set framing, no production teaching.
- All four photographs are new to the site (md5 compared against every other `img/` directory) and openly licensed.
- The Ultra Europe 18+ age limit is now confirmed: ultraeurope.com/tickets says "Ages 18+ only". Add that page to Sources.

## 3. Priority revisions

### Blockers

**B1. The "biggest" claim has no source.** Neither the site's Ultra guide nor ultraeurope.com says it is the biggest in Croatia. The Ultra guide does give "more than 150,000 people" in 2023, which can be compared with Sea Star's "almost 50,000".
- L27. Old: "Ultra Europe is the biggest electronic festival in the country." New: "Ultra Europe is the biggest festival on this list: it reported more than 150,000 people in 2023."
- FAQ L116. Old: "Ultra Europe in Split is the biggest electronic festival in the country. Its 2027 edition is on 9 to 11 July at Park Mladeži, for ages 18 and over." New: "Ultra Europe in Split is the biggest festival on this list, with more than 150,000 people reported in 2023. Its 2027 edition is on 9 to 11 July at Park Mladeži, for ages 18 and over."

**B2. The Sonus claim is unsupported.** zrce.com says the 2026 edition was cancelled and that no 2027 date has been announced. It says nothing about a move to a new location.
- L101. Old: "The 2026 edition at Zrće was cancelled, and the festival says it will move to a new location for 2027 that it has not yet named." New: "The 2026 edition at Zrće was cancelled, and no 2027 date has been announced."

**B3. Hideout and Hospitality overlap; they do not follow one another.** Hideout runs 29 June to 2 July and Hospitality 30 June to 5 July.
- L108. Old: "Hideout, Hospitality on the Beach and Ultra Europe follow one another in the first half of July, so a long trip can take in more than one." New: "Hideout and Hospitality on the Beach overlap at the start of July, and Ultra Europe follows on 9 to 11 July, so a long trip can take in more than one."

### Major

**M1. "Almost every week" overstates.** Tisno has nothing between 26 July and 19 August.
- L5. Old: "and the small town of Tisno hosts a different festival almost every week." New: "and the small town of Tisno hosts a run of festivals from late June to the end of August."

**M2. "All of them" has no source.** Hideout says it uses five open-air venues.
- L37. Old: "Hideout is the festival that uses all of them." New: "Hideout uses five of them."

**M3. The Ultra photograph is at Poljud.** The 2015 night shot shows the Poljud Stadium, while the text says the festival has been at Park Mladeži since 2019. The caption should say so.
- Caption. Old: "Ultra Europe in Split in 2015. Photograph: Shadster, CC BY-SA 4.0." New: "Ultra Europe at the Poljud Stadium in Split in 2015, before the move to Park Mladeži. Photograph: Shadster, CC BY-SA 4.0."
- Alt text. Old: "The main stage and crowd at Ultra Europe in Split in 2015" New: "The main stage and crowd at Ultra Europe in the Poljud Stadium, Split, in 2015"

**M4. Dekmantel Selectors is described from memory.** dekmantelselectors.com confirms "11th edition on August 19-23, 2027" at The Garden Resort Tisno. Neither it nor the site's Dekmantel guide says "long sets from DJs known for digging deep".
- L73. Old: "Dekmantel Selectors is the Amsterdam festival's smaller sister event, built around long sets from DJs known for digging deep. The 2027 edition is on 19 to 23 August." New: "Dekmantel Selectors is the Amsterdam festival's sister event in Tisno, a five-day festival at The Garden Resort. The 2027 edition, its 11th, is on 19 to 23 August."
- Table. Old: `'Long DJ sets'` New: `'Dekmantel by the sea'`
- L106. Old: "long DJ sets at Dekmantel Selectors," New: "the Dekmantel crowd at Dekmantel Selectors,"
- Sources: add dekmantelselectors.com to the "own site" line.

**M5. Terminal V is confirmed, not listed, and is not "from Edinburgh".** terminalv.co.uk/croatia says "THURSDAY 15TH - MONDAY 19TH JULY 2027". The site says "BUILT IN SCOTLAND", and the 2027 Scottish edition moves to Glasgow.
- L59. Old: "Terminal V, the techno festival from Edinburgh, holds its Croatian edition in Tisno. It is listed for 15 to 19 July 2027, after a 2026 edition the organisers called its largest so far." New: "Terminal V, the Scottish techno festival, holds its Croatian edition in Tisno. The 2027 edition is on 15 to 19 July, after a 2026 edition the organisers called its largest so far."
- Table. Old: `'Listed 15 to 19 July'` New: `'15 to 19 July'`
- FAQ L120. Old: "Terminal V Croatia (listed 15 to 19 July)" New: "Terminal V Croatia (15 to 19 July)"
- Sources: move Terminal V to the "own site" line (terminalv.co.uk/croatia) and keep Mixmag for the largest-edition claim.

**M6. INmusic 2027 dates may already be out.** inmusicfestival.com/en has a banner, "INmusic dates for 2027 are out!", but no dates could be read. The owner needs to check this by hand before publication. If the dates are published, replace "Not announced (22 to 24 June in 2026)" and the L93 sentence. The festival's own wording for the site is "Isle of Youth" at "the Jarun lake complex".
- L93. Old: "held on the island in Lake Jarun in Zagreb." New: "held on the Isle of Youth at Lake Jarun in Zagreb."

### Minor

**m1. Genre labels with no source.**
- Hideout table. Old: `'House, tech house'` New: `'House, techno'`. Hideout's own Our Story page quotes Mixmag's "house, techno, disco and bass" and Dummy's "house and techno".
- Love International (L51 and table) and Dimensions (L77 and table): neither festival's site lists genres. Keep the labels only if the owner supports them from a festival page or the site's own guides. Otherwise change L51 "and it books house and disco alongside wider electronic music" to "and it runs for a week, with boat parties", and change L77 "It books techno, house, and the experimental end of electronic music." to "It is the last festival of the Tisno summer." (In that case also reword the start of L77 so that it does not say the same thing twice.)
- Outlook Origins L69. Old: "Outlook Origins books bass music, from dub and dubstep to drum and bass and garage." New: "Outlook Origins books UK soundsystem music, from dub to drum and bass." This follows the festival's own phrase "UK soundsystem music in all its multitudes".

**m2. "Love International" L51: "began in Tisno" is not on its page.** The page says it "first took place in Croatia over 20 years ago".
- Old: "which began in Tisno more than 20 years ago," New: "which first took place in Croatia more than 20 years ago,"

**m3. The table is out of date order.** Ultra Europe (9 July) sits above Love International (7 July). Swap the two rows.

**m4. Ultra table setting.** The Ultra guide calls Park Mladeži a stadium ("the Youth Park").
- Old: `'City park'` New: `'Stadium park'`

**m5. Closer.**
- L47. Old: "Each festival brings a different sound, so choosing a Tisno week is choosing a style." New: "Each festival brings a different sound."

**m6. Keyword wedge in How to choose.** L106 opens "The best festivals in Croatia for you depend on the kind of week you want.", which repeats the answer box. The phrase is tracked (volume 10), so this is the owner's call. If the phrase is kept, the sentence can stay. If it is dropped, change it to "Pick the kind of week you want first." and add "best festivals in croatia" elsewhere first, or remove the phrase from `keywords/best-music-festivals-croatia.json`.
