# Editorial review: Italo disco guide (/italo-disco-guide)

Written 2026-11-09 by the agent that wrote the guide, so it is **not independent**. It covers the fact check, the humanizer pass and the SEO review. Stage 6 of TOPIC-RESEARCH.md was not run separately: the owner asked for three guides to be written one after another while using credits carefully ("lets create first 3, not in parallel, write them 1 by 1 use creadits conciuosly").

1. **Verdict:** ready after the owner reads it.
2. **What already works:**
   - The answer box defines Italo disco in one sentence, with place, decade, instruments, language and themes.
   - The naming section keeps the Guardian's Mikulski claim and Wikipedia's correction side by side, instead of choosing one silently.
   - The link to house rests on named records (Kano, Klein + M.B.O., Doctor's Cat, Jago), not on general claims, and hands over to /house-music-guide.
   - "Is Italo disco the same as Euro disco" is answered through the umbrella-term section, so no separate page is needed.
3. **Priority revisions:** none open. Fixed while writing:
   - Removed phrases with no source in the notes: Florence, "Bernhard" and "founder", Lake Garda and cosmic, Roberto Zanetti, Giancarlo, "the Dutch DJ" and "Eurobeat made in Italy".
   - Corrected Gazebo's album to the 1983 album Gazebo, and I-F's mix to "four of them sit together on".
   - Separated the Canada, Germany and spacesynth examples: MasterClass for the labels, Wikipedia for the examples.
   - Moved five figures so none sits directly against a player, and shortened the title to 59 characters.

5. **Humanizer pass** (on the draft, 2026-11-09):
   - No "this guide" and no description of the page itself.
   - The sets are introduced by place and date, with nothing about their length.
   - There are no drum and bass, jungle or breaks asides.
   - No PAA question is used as an empty heading.
6. **SEO:**
   - New page. `keywords/italo-disco.json` targets 14 terms, with Keyword Planner ranges and Ahrefs US figures kept apart.
   - Rejected as file, collection or forum intent: mp3, collection, ZYX collection, forum, net, "2023" and "the lost legends".
   - "disco italo" was dropped because it only reads as a search string.
   - The FAQ answers the four PAA questions in full.
   - Title: "What Is Italo Disco? Italian Disco Music, Songs and Artists".
7. **Coverage gaps:**
   - Reddit was read only as a search snippet, so the 110–125 BPM figure is not used.
   - Mr. Flagio "Take a Chance" and Spagna "Call Me" have no official upload, so they are named without a player.
8. **Media:**
   - Six Wikimedia Commons images, none used by another guide: Giorgio Moroder, Ryan Paris in 2023, Gazebo in 2012, Sabrina Salerno in 2012 (hero and card), Savage in 2014 and Alexander Robotnick at Batofar in 2007.
   - Eleven records from official or label channels and three sets (David Vunk, Daniele Baldelli and Pablo Bozzi). Each passed oEmbed and was checked unused elsewhere.
   - The owner's mix is not used here.
