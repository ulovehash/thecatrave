## 1. Facts and timeline review

- Order of release years is chronological in the page and the lists. The 1998 and 1999 heading holds 1998 (9 PM) and 1999 records; no 2000 or 2001 record is included, so the heading overstates the range. Left as the keyword-bearing heading; flagged for the owner.
- "Nine of the twelve reached the UK singles chart" matches the table (nine rows). Satellite, Communication Part 3 and Concrete Angel are stated as not found on the main chart.
- ASOT order differs between the two pages; the page claims only what holds on both and says so.
- No invented tracks: each of the twelve has a verified official or label upload.

## Independent review

Reviewer did not write the draft. No Ahrefs or Keyword Planner access; volumes not re-measured.

**Verdict:** ship after the corrections below. All audits pass.

**Facts corrected (each logged in defects.json first):**
- Silence number 3 was dated 1999; the October 2000 remixes (Tiesto, Airscape and others) took it to 3.
- Satellite was labelled "dance only"; removed.
- Children was "ninth, 653,000"; now eighth best-selling single of 1996.
- Café del Mar UK peak 24 was attributed to 1997; sources conflict, so the peak is omitted. The Three 'n One remix chart entry is March 1997.
- Out of the Blue "six months promo, February 1999" reworded to what sources support.
- ASOT: the two pages are the January 2021 vote (Shivers first) and the April 2024 vote (RAMsterdam remix first). Adagio is second on both and Silence fourth. The intro now says "top five of both" for Silence and Adagio.
- Table: nine rows sorted by peak, with a source note naming the three left out.

**Claim ledger:** Official Charts song pages, the two ASOT result pages, DJ Mag and YouTube oEmbed were opened and read directly. All 12 embeds resolve to the named track. Adagio is on the Kontor.TV label channel (probably official, minor).

**Humanizer:** cut not-X-but-Y constructions, one-line closers and inflated significance; every measured keyword phrase in title, H2 and FAQ kept word for word.

**SEO:** all 7 keywords in keywords/best-trance-tracks.json present. Volumes are brief-supplied "1K-10K", not measured. Intent is a ranked listening list, distinct from /trance-guide. Read next was irrelevant (Burning Man, Berlin clubs); the home-articles.mjs tags are now ['trance','history'], so it shows the trance, hardstyle, UK electronic and German electronic guides. The side effect is that the 'discovery' homepage filter no longer lists this article.

**Own tracks:** ownTrackListening blocks plus one bandcampSupport block, labelled as the owner's. No breaks or drum and bass angle. The Bandcamp item "Protect Ya Breaks" is a title only.

**Visual:** served statically and opened at 1440, 768 and 390. scrollWidth equals viewport width, 16 embeds present, 0 broken images (lazy images forced eager).

**Checks:** build, check:html, audit-all (23 audits) and check:links all pass. check:layout not run (no Playwright browser).

**Open:** UK peaks for Communication Part 3 and Concrete Angel and the Café del Mar peak not found; some release dates rest on discography pages (medium confidence); Armin photo venue unstated; "1998 and 1999" heading while Silence peaked in 2000 (text explains); no German or French versions; first-person "my pick" voice.
