## History anchors (need a second source before print)

- **Pacha Ibiza** opened June 1973; the first Pacha opened in Sitges in 1967;
  founded by the Urgell brothers, Ricardo and Piti (Ibiza Spotlight "10
  surprising facts about Pacha", Wikipedia "The Pacha Group", RA "Get to
  know: Amnesia and Pacha"). Several sources agree.
- **Amnesia** opened 1976 in San Rafael in an old finca; the island's longest
  running club (Wikipedia, Euronews 2024, RA). Several sources agree.
- **Ku / Privilege**: Ku opened 1979 in San Rafael and later became
  Privilege, which closed after the 2019 season (Wikipedia "Privilege Ibiza").
  The closure date still needs a second source.
- **Space**: current form from 1989 (Pepe Roselló lease). The 2016 closing
  and the site becoming Hï Ibiza are **not yet sourced**. Do not print
  from memory.
- Gray Area, "The Forgotten Clubs of Ibiza": found, not read. A lead for the
  closed clubs section.
- The 1987 Balearic/acid-house link to the UK: `acid-house-guide.html`
  already covers it. Link to it rather than repeat it.
