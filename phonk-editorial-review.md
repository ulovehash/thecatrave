## Must-fix

1. **Wikipedia is named in the body 20 times** (section 0 forbids it). Examples: "Wikipedia notes that the word...", "He explained in an interview, quoted on Wikipedia, that", "Wikipedia calls the label a mislabel" (FAQ). State the facts plainly. Keep the outlet name only for opinion or analysis (The Conversation, Crack, Okayplayer). Example: "He explained in an interview that 'phonk is slang for funk', a nod to G-funk." Drop Wikipedia from the Sources list if no claim depends on it alone. Several do, so qualify those claims.
2. **Unsupported year.** "In 2026 Boiler Room went to Memphis to film him" and the caption "Tommy Wright III at Willie Mitchell's studio in Memphis, 2026." The ledger has no date for the upload. Replace with "Boiler Room filmed him at Willie Mitchell's studio in Memphis for a session called The Legends of Memphis Rap." Caption: "Tommy Wright III at Willie Mitchell's studio in Memphis."
3. **Overclaim.** "Tommy Wright III is one of the Memphis names that recurs in every account." Only Wikipedia names him; The Conversation, Crack, Okayplayer and Epidemic Sound do not. Use: "Tommy Wright III is one of the Memphis names Wikipedia lists among phonk's influences." Same fault: "The phonk artists every account names fall into three groups." Use "The phonk artists named in the sources fall into three groups."
4. **Causal claim not in the source, repeated four times.** "Wikipedia says 'Murder in My Mind' made him the first phonk producer to break into Spotify's top 500", the embed note "The song that took a phonk producer into Spotify's top 500", the song-list line, and the FAQ. Wikipedia says only that he is best known for the single and is recognised as the first phonk producer in the top 500. Use: "Kordhell, best known for 'Murder in My Mind', is recognised as the first phonk producer to reach Spotify's top 500 artists." Also unsupported: "by streams it is Kordhell". The ledger has no stream data. Use: "Who is the most famous phonk artist depends on which phonk you mean. In drift phonk, the name most often given is Kordhell." Soften the FAQ the same way. FAQ also says SpaceGhostPurrp "is the best-known name in the original style, since he coined the word": inference, so use "is the name most tied to the original style".
5. **Wrong grouping.** "The Memphis foundations: Three 6 Mafia, Tommy Wright III, DJ Spanish Fly, DJ Squeeky and X-Raided". Wikipedia says only "Southern United States". X-Raided is a Sacramento rapper, not Memphis, so this is an error. Use "The 1990s foundations Wikipedia names: Three 6 Mafia, Tommy Wright III, DJ Spanish Fly, DJ Squeeky and X-Raided, with Houston's DJ Screw as the precursor." Same fix for "Phonk starts in Memphis... Wikipedia names the artists", where "Memphis" should be "the Southern United States".
6. **Image caption location.** "A drift at ADM Raceway, Festival Drift Expo, Russia, May 2021." Neither the file description nor the ledger places ADM Raceway in Russia. The Commons category says "Motorsports in Russia", the photographer is credited as Polish, and the EXIF name differs. Do not print "Russia" unless verified. Use "A drift at ADM Raceway during the Drift Expo festival, May 2021." The text beside it ties the image to Russian drift phonk, so confirm the venue country or replace the image.
7. **Self-contradiction on Brazilian phonk.** Intro: "the name moved once more, to a Brazilian sound that had little to do with either." Later text and Wikipedia say it combines phonk with local elements, and Epidemic Sound calls it Brazilian funk mixed with drift phonk. Use: "After drift phonk, the name moved once more, to a Brazilian funk style."
8. **Process commentary and housekeeping.** "Interworld's 'Metamorphosis' is named here without a player, because no upload from the artist or a label could be found." and ""Rave" is from DXRK's own channel." Cut both. Keep the single Rave embed with the caption already present.

## Should-fix

"A handful", "young" and "Florida" are not sourced (Epidemic Sound says only that SpaceGhostPurrp was Florida-based). The SoundCloud peak is 2016 to 2018. Use: "The first began in the early 2010s, when SpaceGhostPurrp and his Raider Klan collective went back to the Memphis rap of the 1990s."
