# Creamfields: editorial review

Protocol: `ARTICLE-EDITORIAL-REVIEW.md`. Draft: `creamfields-draft.md`,
13 September 2026. New page, so no Search Console preservation inventory
exists. The review was written by the same session that wrote the draft; the
owner reviews next.

## 1. Verdict

Ready after revisions. The revisions below have been applied to the draft.

## 2. What already works

- The answer block gives what, where, when, capacity, founders and owner in one
  paragraph. The H1 and title carry the primary query, "creamfields festival".
- The attendance table is qualified in the text: the published totals count
  admissions per day, not people. No competitor SERP page explains this.
- The music section argues from evidence: drum and bass on the bill in 2012,
  2016, 2020, 2025 (headlining the Arc) and 2026, with the festival's own
  drum and bass film.
- Dated intents are kept out. Prices appear once, dated to 2026, in the FAQ.

## 3. Priority revisions (applied)

- **Blocker, removed**: "took a coach trip's worth of its crowd to Hampshire".
  Invented.
- **Blocker, corrected**: "Paul van Dyk headlined for most of the 2000s".
  Wikipedia's headliners table has him in 1998 and then 2008 to 2014 (not
  2012). Rewritten to match.
- **Major, corrected**: "at least fifteen years" of drum and bass. The earliest
  sourced booking is Sub Focus on the 2012 Sunday. Changed to "since at least
  2012".
- **Major, corrected**: "the festival Creamfields is most often compared with"
  (Tomorrowland). No source. Replaced with the sourced 2018 Steel Yard Garden
  of Madness tie-up.
- **Major, corrected**: "a hardstyle stage". The source names the Misfit Stage
  and Maddix, not the stage's genre.
- **Major, corrected**: "More have followed" for APEX. No source dates APEX.
  Josh Baker's 2026 Arc debut is phrased as a booking, because the source is
  an announcement.
- **Minor**: "tenth edition" changed to "tenth anniversary" (2008), as in
  Wikipedia.

## 5. SEO preservation

A new page. Every term in `keywords/creamfields.json` must survive editing, and
`audit-keywords.mjs` enforces it once the page is in `pages.mjs`. The terms
most at risk in a later edit, because they read least naturally, are
"creamfields warrington", "creamfields uk", "creamfields steel yard" and
"creamfields capacity".

## 6. Coverage gaps

- PAA "Is Creamfields a rave?" is not answered as its own question. An honest
  answer needs a source on how the festival is licensed and described. "What
  is Creamfields known for?" took its place.
- PAA on alcohol, sleeping in cars and banned items: practical rules that
  change each year, so excluded.
- Creamfields Steel Yard London's dates are not in the page beyond the name.
- The PAA "postcode" question: no official postcode found, so nothing is stated.
