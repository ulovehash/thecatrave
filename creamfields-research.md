## Our catalogue

The Selector's catalogue holds 52 Creamfields sets: 51 from Beatport's streams
(2019, 2023, 2024) and 1 from Mixmag (2016). All are house, tech house and
techno acts. None is by the drum and bass acts the page names. This is not a
criterion (`TOPIC-RESEARCH.md`). It is cited in the page as a number, and it
means the players come from the festival's own channel.

## Decisions

- **URL** `/creamfields-festival`. It has the highest TP of the informational
  forms (13,000), no article in its top ten, and the weakest pages at 0 to
  1 RD. "where is creamfields" has more volume (1,900) but TP 2,700, and its
  SERP is social posts. `/what-is-creamfields` would be the Burning Man
  pattern, but that query is 100 a month.
- **Title** Creamfields Festival: Where It Is, How It Grew, the Music (57).
- **H1** Creamfields Festival.
- **Meta** Where Creamfields happens in Cheshire, how a Liverpool house night
  became a four-day festival, how many people go, who owns it, and what plays
  beyond the Arc Stage.

Structure, mirroring Tomorrowland:

1. Answer block ("What is Creamfields"): what, where, when, how big, owner.
2. Where Creamfields happens: Daresbury, Warrington postal address,
   distances, the bank holiday rule, the earlier sites. H3 Creamfields South,
   H3 Creamfields around the world.
3. How big Creamfields is: capacity, attendance table (Wikipedia weekend
   totals, qualified as admissions), the 2015 livestream.
4. A short history, and who owns Creamfields: Cream at Nation, 1998
   Winchester, Speke, Daresbury, Live Nation 2012, the 2012 flood, 2020 to
   2021, the 2026 storms.
5. Why Creamfields is famous: Arc, Steel Yard, APEX, HALO; awards.
6. What the music actually is: house origin, trance, techno, then drum and
   bass as the site's angle (2012, 2016, 2020, 2025 Arc headline, 2026 bill).
   Official 2019 drum and bass film as the player.
7. Hearing Creamfields from home: catalogue numbers; Essential listening with
   two full 2025 sets from the festival's channel (Pete Tong, Josh Baker).
8. FAQ (six, from PAA and question terms).
9. Author, Sources, Bandcamp (the owner's two tracks), Read Next.

## Excluded intents

- Line-ups, set times, dated editions, weather forecasts: official site.
- Tickets: one dated FAQ answer.
- Travel, hotels, postcode, outfits, what to bring.
- Alcohol, sleeping in cars, what is not allowed: practical rules that change
  from year to year.
