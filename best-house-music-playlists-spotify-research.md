# Best house music playlists on Spotify: research package and decision gate

Research date: 29 September 2026.

## Decision

Create a new English page at `/best-house-music-playlists-spotify` for listeners
who want a house-specific Spotify playlist. The page should organise playlists
by musical job and curatorial point of view, not by claimed follower count.

No Search Console preservation inventory exists because this is a new URL.
The existing `/best-spotify-playlists` owns the broad platform-wide query and
contains only a few electronic entries. The new page earns its own URL by going
deeper into house while linking back to the broad guide. It must not repeat the
same introductory argument, FAQ or twelve-playlist list.

## Semantic kernel

- Primary intent: best house music playlists on Spotify, best house playlists
  on Spotify.
- Close secondary intent: curated house playlists, classic house playlist,
  deep house playlist, soulful house playlist, tech house playlist and current
  house releases, only where the selected playlist genuinely covers that lane.
- Required entities: Spotify, curator, playlist title, direct playlist ID,
  update cadence checked on the research date, opening tracks and the lane the
  playlist actually occupies.
- Comparison questions: broad versus focused, label versus artist versus
  editorial curator, classics versus current releases.
- Excluded: playlist submission, promotion, follower manipulation, production,
  a house history section and a claim to rank playlists by popularity.

## Proposed metadata

- URL: `/best-house-music-playlists-spotify`
- SEO title: `Best House Music Playlists on Spotify: 12 Curated Picks`
- H1: `The best house music playlists on Spotify`
- Meta description: `Twelve Spotify house playlists with a clear curatorial point of view, from Chicago classics and soulful house to deep, tech and current club music.`
- Direct-answer label: `BEST HOUSE MUSIC PLAYLISTS ON SPOTIFY`

The count is provisional until the playlist IDs, curators, opening tracks and
update state are checked directly.

## Proposed outline

1. Hero and direct answer: the shortest route to a playlist for a reader's
   preferred kind of house.
2. **How these house playlists were chosen**: identifiable curator, coherent
   lane, active or durably useful selection, sequencing and no paid-placement
   claim masquerading as editorial quality.
3. **House foundations and classics**: playlists grounded in Chicago, New York,
   Detroit and the genre's Black and Latin roots.
4. **Deep, soulful and melodic house**: keep distinct lanes visible rather than
   treating them as synonyms.
5. **Tech house and current club records**: label or editorial feeds with a
   clear update purpose.
6. **Independent and artist-curated routes**: strong selections whose value is
   the curator's ear rather than scale. Any thecatrave playlist appears here,
   uses the same criteria and carries an explicit ownership disclosure.
7. **Which house playlist should you choose?**: comparison table by lane,
   curator and best use.
8. Author, Sources, Bandcamp support, Read Next and footer.

No FAQ is proposed until query evidence shows a question that the selection and
comparison table do not already answer. “What is house music?” belongs to the
house guide, not this page.

## Selection and media plan

- Every entry uses `articlePlaylistPreview()` with the exact official Spotify
  playlist ID, curator, direct link and one editorial paragraph.
- The final list should include institutional, label, artist and independent
  curators. No publisher or network receives several slots solely because it
  owns them.
- Verify the first visible tracks and update state on the same date. Avoid exact
  follower figures unless they materially help and can be maintained.
- Use one comparison table from `articleTable()`.
- Use at most one original or openly licensed image that says something about
  house listening or record culture. Do not add a generic festival crowd.
- Text separates the image, playlist previews and Bandcamp stripe.

## Material uncertainties before drafting

- The live SERP's largest curated article is heavily self-owned. Every candidate
  must therefore be checked independently rather than inherited from its list.
- Spotify titles, curators and opening tracks can change. The page needs a
  visible checked date and should avoid volatile follower-count rankings.
- The owner requested both broader thecatrave electronic playlists be included.
  They appear in a separate disclosed section, not as house-only selections,
  because both contain techno, breaks and other electronic styles alongside
  house and house-adjacent records.
