# Berghain editorial review

Reviewed 2026-10-04 against `berghain-draft.md`, `build-berghain-article.mjs` and the built `berghain.html`. Not independent: the same session gathered the evidence (Stage 6 not run by a second party).

## 1. Verdict
Ready after revisions: none blocking. Several facts are unverifiable from first-party sources; the page states each gap instead of filling it (section 9).

## 2. What already works
- Direct answer in the banner: where the club is, how it is organised, the next Klubnacht as listed on 4 October 2026, age limit, and what the official site does not publish.
- Every date, price and line-up carries its read date (4 October 2026).
- Where Mixmag, Groove and Crack disagree (Panorama Bar sound system, Ostgut Ton dates) the page says so and does not pick a side.
- The Klubnacht counts are labelled as the author's own count of listed names, not an official resident list.
- Dress code, queue times, Klubnacht price and closing time are stated as not published, not guessed.

## 3. Priority revisions
None Blocker or Major.
- Minor: the resident sets in the sound section are titled "Boiler Room Berlin"; no source says they were recorded at Berghain. The page says so.
- Minor: the FAQ entry on the dress code mainly points back to its section. It exists to give the FAQ five entries (audit-site-components requires at least five).

## 5. SEO preservation
New page. All 21 terms in `keywords/berghain.json` appear verbatim (audit-keywords passes: 951 terms across 183 pages). Title "Berghain: Panorama Bar, Sound and Residents" and the approved meta are used unchanged. FAQ text matches the FAQPage data.

## 6. Coverage gaps
Dress code, door behaviour, queue times, Klubnacht entry price, closing time and an official resident list are not published by the club. The page says so. Panorama Bar's sound system is disputed between two sources and left undescribed.

## 7. Cuts or merges
Door stories are deliberately not repeated; the page points to /best-clubs-in-berlin. FAQ answers repeat section openings, acceptable for FAQ.
