## Differentiation (what the page does that the ranking pages do not)

- Separates the sound, the Chicago genre label and the British movement.
- Tells the Chicago origin from DJ Pierre's own account (RBMA 2012) and DJ
  Mag (2014), with the disputed dating (late 1985 or 1986) left open.
- Handles "first acid record" as a dispute: Sleezy D on vinyl first (1986),
  Acid Tracks played first and named the sound.
- Three explanations of the name, including the "acid burning" story a rave
  publicist later said he invented.
- The India question answered as "same machine, reached first, no line of
  influence", not as an invention claim.
- Nine exact records embedded beside the prose that argues about them.
