# Best SoundCloud DJ mixes: research package and decision gate

Research date: 29 September 2026.

## Owner mix disclosure update

The page includes `I Like to Smoke in Silence After Raves` as a ninth, separately labelled personal pick. It is not counted among the eight independent editorial selections. The relationship appears before the player, and the copy says the mix is not being claimed as one of the best, only as worth the reader's time.

## Decision

Create a new English page at `/best-soundcloud-dj-mixes` for listeners who want
complete DJ mixes they can play on SoundCloud now. The page is a judged,
cross-genre selection of individual mixes, not a directory of accounts and not
a guide to uploading, promoting or downloading DJ mixes.

No Search Console preservation inventory exists because this is a new URL.
The closest existing pages are `/live-dj-sets`, which explains the platforms
that film and stream sets, and `/best-boiler-room-sets`, which is restricted to
one video platform. This page must not repeat either page's history or rankings.

## Semantic kernel

- Primary intent: best SoundCloud DJ mixes, SoundCloud DJ mixes.
- Close secondary intent: mixes on SoundCloud, DJ sets on SoundCloud, electronic
  music mixes on SoundCloud.
- Required entities: SoundCloud, the mix uploader or commissioning series, each
  DJ, mix title, upload date, genre and direct track URL.
- Reader questions: what the mix sounds like, why this recording earns a place,
  who uploaded it and where to continue if the reader likes it.
- Excluded: uploading a mix, copyright advice, DJ software integration,
  downloads, follower rankings and a general history of SoundCloud.

## Proposed metadata

- URL: `/best-soundcloud-dj-mixes`
- SEO title: `Best SoundCloud DJ Mixes: 12 Sets Worth Hearing`
- H1: `The best SoundCloud DJ mixes worth hearing`
- Meta description: `Twelve SoundCloud DJ mixes selected for sequencing, range and replay value, with direct players and a clear route through house, techno, bass and leftfield club music.`
- Direct-answer label: `BEST SOUNDCLOUD DJ MIXES`

The number 12 is provisional until every mix, uploader and direct player has
been verified. The title should lose the number rather than pad the list if
fewer than 12 survive fact-checking.

## Proposed outline

1. Hero and concise direct answer: what qualifies and how the list differs from
   an activity chart.
2. **How these SoundCloud mixes were chosen**: legitimate uploader, playable
   full recording, identifiable editorial or artist context, strong sequencing
   and a distinct job for the listener.
3. **House and groove-led mixes**: individual H3 entries with direct players.
4. **Techno and high-pressure mixes**: individual H3 entries, without absorbing
   the separate techno canon page.
5. **Bass, breaks and leftfield mixes**: individual H3 entries.
6. **Which SoundCloud mix should you play first?**: compact comparison table by
   sound, energy and entry point.
7. Author, concise Sources, Bandcamp support, Read Next and footer.

No FAQ is proposed yet. The free result inspection did not reveal stable,
in-intent questions that are not better answered in the body.

## Selection and media plan

- Every recommendation uses the exact SoundCloud track through
  `articleListeningBand()` or a shared multi-entry extension if the final layout
  proves that twelve full-width bands are too heavy.
- A mix qualifies only when the artist, commissioning series or other credible
  rights holder uploaded it. Reposts with unclear provenance are rejected.
- Each entry states why the sequencing or selection is distinctive. Runtime is
  factual context, never the selling point.
- Text separates every player. No player is placed directly beside a figure.
- Use at most one or two openly licensed photographs if they add evidence about
  a mix series or recording context. Do not add generic crowd photography.
- The final Bandcamp stripe remains separate from the editorial selection and
  discloses the relationship to thecatrave.

## Material uncertainties before drafting

- Verify that every candidate remains playable and embeddable from the original
  or commissioning account.
- Decide whether the final selection should be durable rather than current. The
  recommendation is durable, with a visible checked date, and no year in the
  URL or H1.
- Confirm the final genre balance after direct listening. Do not reserve a slot
  for a genre if no mix meets the same standard.
