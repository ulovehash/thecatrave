# Editorial review: Best Electronic Music Festivals in Asia

Reviewed 4 October 2026 against `ARTICLE-EDITORIAL-REVIEW.md` and the format of `time-warp-editorial-review.md`.

## 1. Verdict

Ready to publish as a first edition. A choosing guide for seven festivals with the 2027 status of each stated from its own site where it could be read. Only Ultra Japan has an official 2027 date; the rest say "not announced" rather than carrying a guessed date.

## 2. What already works

- The answer paragraph names the festivals and separates the two with 2027 dates from the rest.
- The at-a-glance table gives the 2027 column its own place.
- Every price and date has a named source in the text and in the Sources block.
- Ultra Korea is kept with its real status (latest edition 20 September 2025, none listed after).
- Five new Commons images, credited in captions; four catalogue sets (three Ultra Korea, one Wonderfruit); the two owner mixes.

## 3. Priority revisions

1. **Updated 5 October 2026:** Zamna now lists Phuket, but its official event page contradicts itself: date field 29 January 2027, description 23 and 24 January. The published guide gives the month and directs readers to confirm ticket dates before booking travel.
2. Add Wonderfruit, Sunburn and DWP 2027 dates after their December 2026 editions.
3. Add S2O 2027 dates when the festival announces them (spring 2027 expected, not confirmed).
4. Replace the Go2Thailand and EDM Addicts figures with official ones if those sites become readable.

## 5. SEO preservation

Terms and volumes (Keyword Planner ranges, lower bound) are in `keywords/best-electronic-music-festivals-asia.json`; every term appears in the built page and `audit-keywords` passes. The title keeps the intent term (no measured volume) and the H2s carry the festival names (10K to 100K for Ultra Japan, Wonderfruit, S2O, Sunburn). TAKEN terms (tomorrowland thailand, lollapalooza india, electronic music festivals in europe) are not targeted; Tomorrowland Thailand appears once as a fact.

## 6. Coverage gaps

- No Ultra Japan, S2O, Sunburn, DWP or Zamna set: none in `selector-data.json`.
- Other Japanese, Indian and Chinese festivals were not researched, and the page says so.

## 7. Cuts or merges

- Ultra Singapore: no edition since 2019; moved to "Not on this list".
- Storm: no official site or named press; dropped and noted.
- Third-party S2O 2027 dates: not repeated, no festival source.
- Lollapalooza India: not claimed.
