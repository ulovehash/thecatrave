# Editorial review: best-edm-festivals-usa (2 October 2026)

Method: humanizer skill run on `best-edm-festivals-usa-draft.md`. Every measured keyword phrase, fact, date, rank and proper name was kept.

## Tells found and fixed

- **Repeated closers (§2, §7).** Nearly every festival block ended "It suits ...". Kept for the festivals where the clause adds information (Ultra, EDC Orlando, Beyond Wonderland, Lost Lands, Bass Canyon, Electric Forest); varied or cut for EDC Las Vegas, HARD Summer, CRSSD, III Points, Dreamstate and Decadence.
- **Unsupported rider (§15, §23).** The smaller-festivals lead ("each with a reason to travel") promised something the Ubbi Dubbi entry does not give. Replaced by a plain statement that the three fall outside the groups above.
- **Fantasy theme and "easiest to reach" (§16).** Beyond Wonderland carried an unsourced theme and an unsourced claim; both removed earlier in drafting.
- **Unsourced trance claim.** "Few festivals in the US built around their music" removed.
- **Staging.** No dive-in openers, no "not X but Y" used for weight, no dashes, no bold labels, no triads beyond real three-item lists.

## Left as is, on purpose

- The contrast "a city festival rather than a field" (Ultra) and "not one stage among rock and pop headliners" (criteria) answer a real distinction in the roster.
- The "How to choose" sequence (sound, setting, date) has three real parts.
- Date and "not announced" repetition: it is the point of the page.

- Bonnaroo cancellation: EDM Sauce only, attributed in the text.
- HARD Summer genres: Wikipedia only, attributed in the text.
