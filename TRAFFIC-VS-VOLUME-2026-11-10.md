# Traffic vs main-keyword volume: top 50 pages (10 November 2026)

What a page actually earns from search, set against what Keyword Planner says its
main keyword is worth. Use it to read a Keyword Planner bucket as a forecast.

## Sources

- Traffic: Search Console, web search, last 28 days, exported 30 October 2026
  (`~/Downloads/thecatrave-15/Pages.csv`), scaled to a month (×30.4/28). Home,
  Selector and the articles index left out.
- Main keyword: the `seed` and first `terms` entry in `keywords/<page>.json`; the
  higher-volume of the two is shown.
- Volume: Google Ads Keyword Planner, Get search volume, all locations, all
  languages, last 12 months, run 10 November 2026. Buckets only. Four terms came
  back merged as close variants (electronic music festivals in europe, new year's
  eve festivals, defqon.1, boiler room set).
- Ratio: monthly clicks divided by the top and the bottom of the bucket.

## What it shows

1. **Festival and club round-ups beat their head keyword.** The Europe guide takes
   about 1,080 clicks a month on a main keyword Keyword Planner puts at 100–1K, and
   the USA guide takes 340 on another 100–1K term. Search Console impressions (36,900 and
   14,100) are far above the bucket, so the page collects many variants the head
   term does not count. For this page type the head bucket is a floor, not a ceiling.
2. **Translated pages earn traffic Keyword Planner cannot see.** All eleven
   German, Spanish and French round-up terms returned no data, yet those pages take
   11 to 111 clicks a month. A "no data" result in another language is not zero.
3. **Single-festival, club and brand pages earn a tiny slice of their head
   volume.** Tomorrowland, Berghain, Electric Forest, Sziget, Boomtown, Dekmantel,
   Defqon.1 and Parookaville sit at 10K to 10M and convert at well under 1%. The
   head query is navigational; the official site takes it. Their bucket says
   nothing about the traffic a guide will get.
4. **Genre heads are the same.** Jungle music and breakbeat (10K–100K) give 26 to
   59 clicks a month at position 6 to 7, under 0.6% of the bucket.
5. **"Best X" lists in 1K–10K land at roughly 0.1% to 2%**, lower still past
   position 9 (Berlin, NYC and Prague clubs, Spotify playlists).

## Caveats

- One 28-day window (2 to 29 October). Festival pages are seasonal; the Europe
  guide was in its first strong month.
- Buckets span ten times, so each ratio is a range, not a number.
- The main keyword is the one the page was written for, not necessarily the
  query that brings its clicks. Search Console's per-page query split was not
  pulled.
