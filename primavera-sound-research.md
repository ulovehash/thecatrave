## Search intent and opportunity

The useful intent is an evergreen English guide to Primavera Sound in Barcelona:
what it is, where it takes place, its dates, what music it represents, how big
it is, and how the Barcelona edition relates to Porto and Primavera a la Ciutat.
The page structure follows those measured keyword clusters and accepted People
Also Ask questions. History is supporting context inside the entity answer, not
a standalone search-led section.

The main competing pages split the answer:

| Page type | What it does well | Gap for The Cat Rave |
|---|---|---|
| Official Primavera Sound site | Current programme, ticketing and announcements | Dynamic and transactional; weak as a stable explanatory guide |
| Wikipedia | Long chronology, editions, artists and attendance | Reference-heavy rather than editorial; limited practical orientation and listening path |
| Barcelona tourism/event guides | Dates, venue and basic logistics | Dated quickly; little music identity or history |
| Reddit first-timer threads | First-hand questions about access, age, stages and atmosphere | Fragmented, edition-specific and not authoritative |

The opening is therefore not another line-up or ticket page. It is a sourced,
evergreen festival profile with a short current 2027 answer, a clear history,
and embedded music that lets a reader hear the festival's range.

## Facts settled for drafting

- Primavera Sound began in Barcelona in 2001. It moved from Poble Espanyol to
  Parc del Fòrum in 2005.
- Parc del Fòrum is an urban waterfront venue rather than a camping festival
  site. The Barcelona agreement keeps the event there through 2030.
- The announced main Barcelona dates for 2027 are 3, 4 and 5 June. This is the
  only year-led query intentionally kept in the structure.
- Reported attendance needs attribution and definition. The 2026 edition drew
  287,000 visits across the programme; edition totals are not the same thing as
  simultaneous capacity or unique people.
- The festival grew from an indie-rock identity into a broad programme spanning
  pop, hip-hop, electronic music and experimental work. Avoid reducing it to an
  EDM festival.
- Primavera a la Ciutat extends the programme into Barcelona venues. Primavera
  Pro is the professional conference strand. Porto is a separate edition and
  should not be confused with the Barcelona event.
- Ticket age rules are more nuanced than "18+": younger attendees can enter the
  main festival under the official conditions, while some club events are 18+.
