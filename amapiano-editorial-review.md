# Editorial review: amapiano guide (/amapiano-guide)

Written 2026-11-09 by the agent that wrote the guide, so it is **not independent**. It covers the fact check, the humanizer pass and the SEO review. Stage 6 of TOPIC-RESEARCH.md was not run separately: the owner asked for the next three guides to be written one after another while using credits carefully ("next 3", following "lets create first 3, not in parallel, write them 1 by 1 use creadits conciuosly").

1. **Verdict:** ready after the owner reads it.
2. **What already works:**
   - The answer box names the place, the decade, the parent sounds, the two signature elements and what the name means.
   - The page explains the spread (WhatsApp, minibus taxis, free releases, then the major labels) instead of only listing hits.
   - The styles table covers eight named strands, each with a place and a record or name.
   - The house comparison answers "amapiano vs house" searches with sourced facts only.
3. **Priority revisions:** none open. Fixed while writing:
   - Removed claims with no source in the notes: minibus taxis carrying "most" commuters, "Lagos" (now Nigeria), FruityLoops as "cheap", Kabza as "the best-known producer" (now one of the major figures), the Scorpion Kings as the duo who "did most", JaySax as a saxophonist, and an unsourced description of house's four-to-the-floor and disco roots.
   - The log drum is described as a deep, percussive bass hit carrying the bassline, matching Wikipedia's wording, not as a pitched drum.
   - "Umshove" has no player: the upload found credits Leehleza, and the credit could not be confirmed. "Trust Fund" has no official upload. Both are named in the text.
   - MFR Souls' "Amanikiniki" was dropped: no source in the notes mentions it.
   - The Evening Standard is linked through the Yahoo syndication that was actually read, not a guessed standard.co.uk URL.

5. **Humanizer pass** (on the draft, 2026-11-09):
   - No "this guide" and no description of the page itself.
   - The sets are introduced by place, with nothing about their length.
   - There are no drum and bass, jungle or breaks asides.
   - No PAA question is used as an empty heading.
   - Nothing teaches production: FruityLoops and the log drum are named as history and sound, never as technique.
6. **SEO:**
   - New page. `keywords/amapiano.json` targets 9 terms, with Keyword Planner ranges and Ahrefs US figures kept apart. Each one appears verbatim on the page.
   - Rejected: download, mp3, fakaza and tubidy terms (file intent), "amapiano instrumental" and "ama piano beats" (production intent), and "amapiano mix" (mix intent, which YouTube answers).
   - Keyword Planner flags "amapiano" at −90% year on year. The volume is still 10K–100K, but the trend should be rechecked before the guide is refreshed.
   - The FAQ answers eight questions in full.
   - Title: "What Is Amapiano? South African House Music, Songs, Artists".

   - Sgija is mentioned by Mixmag but not described, so it is left out of the table.
