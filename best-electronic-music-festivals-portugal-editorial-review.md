# Editorial review: best-electronic-music-festivals-portugal

Reviewed 3 and 4 November 2026 against `best-electronic-music-festivals-portugal-draft.md`, `build-portugal-festivals-article.mjs` and the built `best-electronic-music-festivals-portugal.html`. The reviewer did not write the guide and did not gather its keyword or topic evidence. Stage 6 (topic research validation) was run separately as the "Third wave: stage 6 verdict (3 November 2026)" in `TOPIC-DOSSIERS.md`, which approved this page as candidate 4 with a narrower brief: budget on "portugal electronic music festival", Boom as one linked entry, no NOS Alive or Primavera entries. The draft keeps to that brief. Any edit after this review needs a re-review.

## 1. Verdict

**Ready after revisions (applied below).** The roster, the venues and five of the seven date lines are right. Two date lines were not:

- Neopop 2027 is not "not announced". The festival's own ticket listing on Xceed ("By Neopop, Made Of You Group") sells 2027 passes for Thursday 5 August, 19:00, to Sunday 8 August, 10:00, early bird from 95 euros. That is the organiser publishing the dates, so 5 to 7 August can be printed as the 2027 date. Techno Airlines, the draft's only source, is an affiliate aggregator and is dropped.
- Sónar Lisboa's "would return in 2027" is out of date as the whole story. The promise is real (organiser email quoted by Echo Boomer, 26 November 2025), but sonarlisboa.pt now shows only "Thank you to everyone who made Sónar Lisboa what it was", a page last changed 10 April 2026. No 2027 date exists anywhere official. The page must say both things.

There is no drum and bass angle, no jungle aside, no dnb line in the Bandcamp card, no whole-set framing and no production teaching.

## 2. What already works

- The intro deals with NOS Alive and Primavera in one sentence and says what the page covers instead, which is the stage 6 brief.
- Boom is one short linked entry and the psytrance guide keeps the detail. Its dates (18 to 25 July 2027) and sell-out agree with `best-psytrance-festivals.html`.
- RFM Somnii (9 to 11 July 2027, 13th edition, Praia do Relógio) and Suncébeat New Horizons (17 to 21 June 2027) are confirmed by the organiser's radio station and by Echo Boomer.
- Both photographs are new to the site, openly licensed and correctly credited (checked on the Commons API, below).
- The H2s are questions people search, each answered in the first line beneath.
