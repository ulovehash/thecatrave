## 1. Verdict

Ready after revisions. The original pre-layout revisions and the approved

## 2. What already works

- The two biggest evergreen questions, when Glastonbury 2027 is and why there
  was no festival this year, have their own sections before anything else,
  with the official dates and the fallow-year history.
- The headliners-by-year table covers every edition from 1970 to 2025 and
  marks the fallow and cancelled years, which answers both "headliners by
  year" and "how often is Glastonbury".
- Attendance separates tickets sold (142,000) from the numbers on site and the
  gatecrasher years.
- Dance music enters through its own sourced history (the 1989 sound systems,
  Orbital in 1994, the dance tent and Dance Village); no drum and bass added
  (`festivals-series.md`).

## 3. Priority revisions (applied)

- Major, 2026-09-15: replaced the Wikipedia-derived attendance wording with the
  current licensed maximum of 210,000 people on site, explicitly distinguishing
  that total from public tickets.
- Major, 2026-09-15: corrected the organiser description. Glastonbury Festival
  Events Ltd stages the festival; Glastonbury Festivals Ltd manages the site and
  infrastructure; Michael and Emily Eavis are directors of the events company.
- Major, 2026-09-15: qualified the disputed 1994 and 2000 crowd estimates rather
  than presenting 300,000 and 250,000 as counted facts.
- Major, 2026-09-15: clarified that the Pyramid Stage hosts the three principal
  headliners, because other stages also have their own headliners.
- Major: "the London Olympics took the police, toilets and attention" came
  from memory; now only what Wikipedia says, that the Olympics moved the
  fallow sequence.
- Major: "the festival had not announced its 2028 plans" was unchecked; cut.
- Major: "the best known festival in Britain" was an unsourced superlative;
  now Wikipedia's "a major event in British culture".
- Minor: "most people arrive by coach or train" overstated the source; now
  what the transport section says. Creamfields is no longer called a late
  summer festival next to a June one. The "legends" slot is described as
  Wikipedia does, without "afternoon".
