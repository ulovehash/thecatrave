# Parookaville: editorial review

Protocol: `ARTICLE-EDITORIAL-REVIEW.md`. Draft: `parookaville-draft.md`,
13 September 2026. New page, so no Search Console preservation inventory
exists. The review was written by the same session that wrote the draft; the

## 1. Verdict

Ready after revisions. The revisions below have been applied to the draft and
the generator.

## 2. What already works

- The answer block gives what, where, when, how big, founders and the city
  concept in one paragraph. The title and H1 carry "parookaville festival", the
  only English-form listener term with volume.
- The admissions table is qualified in the text: the totals count each day's
  entry, which is why 75,000 tickets become 225,000. No page in the top ten
  explains this in English.
- The city concept, the weddings and the stages are described from the
  festival's own pages and de.wikipedia, not from impressions.
- The music section is honest about the site's angle: drum and bass is a guest
  (Pendulum in 2026), and the page says so rather than inflating it.
- Dated intents are kept out. Prices appear once, dated to 2025, in the FAQ.

## 3. Priority revisions (applied)

- **Blocker, replaced**: Essential listening first paired W&W 2022 with Armin
  van Buuren 2024 as "the most watched on an artist's channel". The 2025
  playlist, read after, showed Steve Aoki 2025 at about 1.7 million on his own
  channel. Replaced with Steve Aoki; Armin moved to the known gaps.
- **Blocker, removed**: the attendance table's "daily" column. de.wikipedia

  for 2024 in its text. The page uses tickets and total admissions only.
- **Major, corrected**: "their sets lead the channel's 2023 and 2024 playlists"

  watched".
- **Major, removed**: "the rapper Finch" and "German dance-pop acts". No fetched
  source describes either act. The sentence now gives only the view counts.
- **Major, corrected**: "then called Next Events" in the FAQ implied that Next

  named separately.
- **Major, corrected**: "Rotterdam's gabber". The Paul Elstak source says
  Dutch hardcore and gabber; Rotterdam is not in it. Now "Dutch gabber".
- **Minor**: "nominated at the European Festival Awards every year from 2015
  to 2019" softened to "in the years from 2015 to 2019", as de.wikipedia gives
  it.
- **Minor**: "Bill's Factory, now its second stage by size" had no source for

- **Minor**: "Burning Man as the other" implied only two models; Nordevents

- **Minor**: the Bandcamp copy said the festival "has started to make room for
  drum and bass", a trend from one booking. Now names the 2026 Pendulum

## 5. SEO preservation

A new page. Every term in `keywords/parookaville.json` must survive editing,
and `audit-keywords.mjs` enforces it. The terms most at risk in a later edit,
because they read least naturally, are "parookaville weeze" and "parookaville
location". The page answers the German evergreen questions (where, how many
visitors, what is special) in English; the German phrases are deliberately not
written in.

## 6. Coverage gaps

- PAA "Wo findet Parookaville 2026 statt?" is dated; the where section answers
  it with the mid-July rule and the 2026 dates.
- PAA "Was ist das besondere am Parookaville Festival?" is the famous section
  rather than an FAQ entry, to avoid duplicating it.
- The festival's own figures for daily capacity before 2024 are not stated,
  because the two Wikipedias disagree.
- A German-language page would reach the other 86% of the demand; that is the
  owner's decision (see the research file).
