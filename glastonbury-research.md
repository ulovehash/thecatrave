On 2026-09-15 a factual audit against current primary and authoritative sources
identified four material corrections without changing the approved search
direction:

- Somerset Council's 2025 scrutiny report states that the current licence allows
  up to 210,000 people, rather than more than 210,000. Associated Press clarifies
  that this on-site total includes ticket holders, staff and performers.
- Glastonbury Festival's current company statement says Glastonbury Festival
  Events Ltd stages the festival while Glastonbury Festivals Ltd manages the
  site and infrastructure. Michael and Emily Eavis are directors of the events
  company.
- The frequently repeated 300,000 estimate for the Levellers in 1994 is not a
  counted audience. Contemporary reporting put the 2000 total at about 200,000
  against a 105,000 licence, while later retrospectives commonly cite 250,000.
- The Pyramid Stage hosts the three principal festival headliners; other stages
  also have their own headliners.

## Facts gathered (Wikipedia, the official site)

A five-day festival of contemporary performing arts at Worthy Farm, between
Pilton and Pylle in Somerset, six miles east of Glastonbury, with the Tor in
view; around 200,000 people (more than 210,000 in 2023, capacity 210,000 in
2022); 300,000 in 1994 with gatecrashers. Founded by Michael Eavis, who saw
Led Zeppelin at the 1970 Bath Festival; the first festival, then the Pilton
Festival, in 1970. Run by Glastonbury Festivals Ltd, Michael and Emily Eavis;
Festival Republic managed logistics 2002 to 2012; independent since. Mostly
volunteers; profits to charity (the article names some 2,000 stewards
organised by Oxfam and a field run by Greenpeace). Fallow years about every
five years: 1988, 1991, 1996, 2001, 2006, 2012 (the Olympics), 2018; 2020 and
2021 cancelled; 2026 the first planned fallow year in eight years, announced
during 2025. Pyramid Stage 25 metres tall; the Other Stage; the Green Fields,
Stone Circle, the late-night south-east corner; the John Peel stage renamed
Woodsies in 2023. Coldplay hold the record of five headline sets (2024).
