## History anchors (need a second source before print)

- **RoXY**, opened 1987, strict door and theme nights; burned down 1999
  (VPRO 3voor12 "Heimwee naar Club 11"; Rozenberg Quarterly "High
  Amsterdam: van RoXY tot regelgeving"; Vice "Amsterdam's Most Legendary
  Clubs"). Two sources agree on 1987 and 1999.
- **Club 11** opened 2004 after the post-RoXY lull; predecessor of Trouw and
  De School (3voor12).
- **De School** open 2016 until final closure in January 2024; same operator
  as the closed Trouw (Wikipedia "De School"; single source so far).
- **Melkweg** described as the first concert hall to programme house music
  regularly, with DJ Per as a Saturday resident (search summary of Vice/
  3voor12, not read in full). Needs the exact source.
- **Paradiso**: a 1960s arts venue in a former church (search summary, needs
  a source).
- **24-hour licences** (Radion, Shelter etc.): Time Out mentions Radion's
  24-hour licence; the city's licensing scheme is not yet sourced.
- Geschiedenislokaal Amsterdam, "Nachtclubs in Amsterdam: van de jaren 80
  tot nu": found, not read. Likely the best single Dutch-language source.
