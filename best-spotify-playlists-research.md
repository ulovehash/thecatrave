# Best Spotify playlists: research and query map

Research date: 21 September 2026.

## Decision

Create a new page at `/best-spotify-playlists` for listeners looking for a useful playlist to play now. The page is a judged editorial selection, not a chart of follower counts and not a guide to playlist pitching.

No Search Console preservation inventory exists because this is a new URL.

## Intent boundary and cannibalisation

Primary job: help a listener choose a good Spotify playlist with a recognisable curator and musical purpose.

Excluded jobs:

- how to create a Spotify playlist;
- how artists submit music or buy promotion;
- the most-followed Spotify playlists by follower count;
- generic `music playlists` navigation;
- how to find new music on Spotify, already owned by `/how-to-find-new-music`.

The new page links to `/how-to-find-new-music` for discovery methods beyond playlists, but does not reproduce that guide's argument or FAQ.

## Editorial rule

A playlist qualifies when it has:

1. a curator or institution the reader can identify;
2. a clear lane rather than a generic mood label;
3. evidence of ongoing or durable curation;
4. a reason to use it that is different from the neighbouring entries.

Follower count is context, not the ranking mechanism. The order is by reader job, not by claimed size.

The two thecatrave playlists from the homepage are included in the electronic and dance section. Their ownership is disclosed in the heading and copy. They use the same component, player geometry and editorial criteria as every other entry.

## Metadata and semantic kernel

- Title: `Best Spotify Playlists: 12 Human-Curated Picks`
- H1: `The best Spotify playlists worth following`
- Meta description: `Twelve Spotify playlists selected for strong curation and music worth hearing, from KEXP and Pitchfork to Four Tet, Bicep and the electronic underground.`
- Canonical: `https://thecatrave.com/best-spotify-playlists`

Primary language: best Spotify playlists, best Spotify playlist.

Close secondary language: good playlists on Spotify, top Spotify playlists, Spotify playlist recommendations, human-curated playlists, independent Spotify playlists, electronic Spotify playlists.

Entities: Spotify, KEXP, Pitchfork, Pigeons & Planes, GemsOnVHS, Bicep, Four Tet, Altar, Toolroom, Danny L Harle, UKF, thecatrave.

## Internal-link plan

- `/how-to-find-new-music`: wider discovery methods outside personalised feeds;
- `/live-dj-sets`: full DJ performances instead of playlists;
- `/selector`: one random full set;
- `/drum-and-bass-guide`, `/uk-garage-guide`, `/dubstep-guide`, `/bass-music-guide`: scene context for the electronic entries.

## Measurement and reversal evidence

Primary outcome: organic entrances to `/best-spotify-playlists` that continue into a Spotify playlist or another thecatrave guide.

Leading signals after indexing: impressions and average position for the primary cluster, CTR for the title, outbound Spotify clicks, and internal continuation to the discovery and genre guides.

Interpretation guardrail: playlist iframe starts are not evidence that a listener liked or followed a playlist.

Revisit the structure if Search Console shows electronic modifiers becoming the dominant query group, or if the broad page begins cannibalising `/how-to-find-new-music` for discovery queries.
