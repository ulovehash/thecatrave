# Best techno mixes: research package and decision gate

Research date: 29 September 2026.

## Decision

Create a new English page at `/best-techno-mixes` as a platform-agnostic guide
to recorded techno DJ mixes that reward complete listening. It should connect
Detroit, radio mix culture, commercial mix CDs and present-day online archives
without turning into another history of techno.

No Search Console preservation inventory exists because this is a new URL.
The existing `/techno-music-guide` owns the genre-definition and history intent;
the new page must link to that guide and restrict itself to recorded mixes,
selection, sequencing and why each recording matters.

## Semantic kernel

- Primary intent: best techno mixes, essential techno mixes.
- Close secondary intent: classic techno DJ mixes, techno mixes of all time,
  new techno mixes where a current recording merits inclusion.
- Required entities: DJ, mix title, recording or release date, series or label,
  format, city or scene when relevant, direct legal player and the musical
  reason for inclusion.
- Comparisons: Detroit versus European lineages, radio mix versus commercial
  mix CD versus club recording, hypnotic/minimal/hard/industrial approaches.
- Excluded: a ranked list of DJs, how to mix techno, techno production, a repeat
  of the genre's full history and a rolling weekly chart.

## Proposed metadata

- URL: `/best-techno-mixes`
- SEO title: `Best Techno Mixes: 12 Essential DJ Sets`
- H1: `The best techno mixes, from Detroit to now`
- Meta description: `Twelve essential techno mixes across radio, mix CDs and club recordings, chosen for selection, sequencing and what each one reveals about the music.`
- Direct-answer label: `BEST TECHNO MIXES`

The count is provisional. Historical balance and verified availability take
priority over reaching twelve entries.

## Proposed outline

1. Hero and direct answer: a concise starting route, selection criteria and a
   link to the existing techno history guide.
2. **What makes a techno mix essential?**: programming, transitions, tension,
   historical position and whether the recording still works as a complete set.
3. **Detroit and the first techno mix language**: early radio, cassette or live
   documents that can be sourced and played legally.
4. **The 1990s and 2000s canon**: mix CDs, Essential Mix broadcasts and club
   recordings, with each format labelled accurately.
5. **Contemporary techno mixes**: a small set of recent recordings that add a
   different technique or regional perspective rather than duplicating older
   entries at a higher tempo.
6. **Where to start**: compact table by style, pressure and format.
7. Author, Sources, Bandcamp support, Read Next and footer.

An FAQ is not proposed at this stage. The visible questions in the live result
set are recommendation prompts that the main list answers directly.

## Candidate listening route and verification rule

The names below are research leads, not approved inclusions: Jeff Mills at the
Liquid Room, a Dave Clarke Essential Mix, a Surgeon mix, Richie Hawtin's DE9
work, Robert Hood, Helena Hauff, Wata Igarashi and one current harder-techno
recording. Each candidate must survive source, date, format and availability
checks. A famous unavailable bootleg does not earn a slot merely because it is
canonical.

- Use the exact official player for each recording through the appropriate
  shared component.
- Label a commercially released mix album as a mix album, a broadcast as a
  broadcast and a filmed club set as a set. Do not flatten all three into
  “live sets”.
- Place entries in a chronological argument, not a countdown.
- Use one or two openly licensed portraits or technology artefacts only where
  they add historical evidence and are not reused from the techno guide.
- Text separates every figure and player.

## Material uncertainties before drafting

- Several canonical commercial mix CDs may not have a legal full-length embed.
  The final list must distinguish editorial importance from playable inclusion.
- Early Detroit radio and cassette documentation needs primary or specialist
  sourcing before any “first” claim.
- The final contemporary selections need direct listening and should not be
  chosen from view counts alone.
