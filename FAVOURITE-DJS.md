# Favourite DJs: reference list

The owner's list, started 3 November 2026. Each entry says who the artist is, how sure the name is (some names came in by voice), which of their recorded sets are in `selector-data.json`, and where to read what they play.

This is a private working file. It is not a guide, and nothing here goes into published copy until it has been checked under `FIGURES.md`.

**Where tracklists are.** The Selector catalogue stores set metadata only (artist, broadcaster, year, length, views, genres) and no tracks. Track IDs come from: the YouTube description and pinned comments of each set below; 1001tracklists.com; set79.com (Boiler Room tracklists); the episode pages on NTS, Rinse and Kiosk Radio; and the printed tracklists in Resident Advisor, Crack, Mixmag and Dekmantel podcast posts.

### Burial
William Bevan, on Hyperdub. Burial has never DJed in public, so there are no club sets to mine. The places where you can hear what he plays are mixes:
- **FABRICLIVE 100** (2018), mixed with Kode9: 37 tracks on the mixed version. Full tracklist in [Mixmag](https://mixmag.net/amp/burial-and-kode9s-fabriclive-mix-drops-with-full-tracklist) and the [RA review](https://ra.co/reviews/23143).
- The Burial and Kode9 mix for Mary Anne Hobbs' last Experimental show on BBC Radio 1.

The only catalogue hit is a tribute show by another DJ: "Dj Saliva - BURIAL & Co" (The Lot Radio, 2025, [link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uop17pnwo2M)).

### Crystal Castles
A band from Toronto, not DJs. **None of their sets are in the catalogue.** What exists is concert footage and a few DJ appearances. If what you want is the music around them, it is better to start from their remixes and the producers they remixed.
