Scope: `/what-is-coachella`, `/live-dj-sets`, and the Barcelona, Paris, Prague, Bristol, Lisbon and Mexico City club guides. This is an optimisation pass on existing URLs, not a new-topic verdict. Existing keyword maps remain the volume record. No new Keyword Planner or paid Ahrefs volume was obtained in this pass, so a missing number below is not zero.

## Limits

The supplied Search Console export had no page rows for these eight URLs. It cannot support an eight-page CTR diagnosis. No new 12-month trend chart, referring-domain count or position-by-query table was measured here. The city SERPs have local packs and change by country and date, so the source checks above validate content choices, not a forecast of ranking. No URL, canonical or primary intent was changed.
